# Physical AI and the pi models

## Abstract

Physical AI is a new field of study that explores the relationship between physics and artificial intelligence. It is a interdisciplinary field that combines ideas from physics, computer science, and mathematics to understand the nature of intelligence and how it can be achieved in physical systems.
