>[!tip]
>Let $n$ be a [[Complex Numbers]] then:
>$$ (1+x)^{n}=1+nx+\frac{n(n-1)}{2!}x^{2}+\dots $$
>As long as $\lvert x \rvert<1$, and you ask why that restriction and [[Radius of Convergence]] appears.

The pattern is:

$$ \binom{n}{k}=\frac{n(n-1)(n-2)\dots(n-k+1)}{k!} $$
