In the study of [[Limits (One Dimension)|limits]] is pretty usual talk about [[Asymptotes (two dimension)]].

Also exist a vast amount of functions where directly evaluate the function at certain points leads to several problems, here is when calculate the limit there is our lifesaver. Free us from various restrictions imposed by the [[Real function]].

Thus the skill of calculate limits it's is an important section. 
We mainly use two tools when things are complicated: the [[L'Hopital's Rule]] and [[Taylor's Polynomial]].
