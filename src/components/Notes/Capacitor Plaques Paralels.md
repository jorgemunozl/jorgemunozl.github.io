Approximate the plaques: So the [[Potential Difference]] $\Delta V$ could be written as:
$$\Delta V=E.d$$
Where $d$ is the distance between the plaques, now what form takes $E$?
 
 Using the [[Gauss Law]] (using a cylinder) you obtain: $$E=\frac{\sigma}{\varepsilon_{o}}$$ Where $\varepsilon_{0}$ is the [[Electrical Permittivity on the Vaccum]] and $\sigma$ is the charge density over the plaques. Thus:
 
  $$\frac{Q}{\Delta V}=\frac{Q}{Ed}=\frac{Q\varepsilon}{\frac{Q}{A}d}=\frac{Q}{ \frac{\sigma}{\varepsilon}d} \\
  =\frac{\varepsilon A}{d}$$
