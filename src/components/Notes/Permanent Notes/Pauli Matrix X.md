In the [[Bloch Sphere, what it is]] this represent a change on the $z$ axis.

$$
\sigma_{x}=\begin{pmatrix}
0&1 \\
1 & 0
\end{pmatrix}
$$

It has [[EigenValues]] $\pm 1$ and [[Eigenvector]]:

$$
\ket{+} =\frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2 }}(\ket{0} +\ket{1} )
$$

$$
\ket{-} =\frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2 }}(\ket{0} -\ket{1} )
$$

Which represents the $X$ axis on the [[Bloch Sphere, what it is|Bloch Sphere]].
