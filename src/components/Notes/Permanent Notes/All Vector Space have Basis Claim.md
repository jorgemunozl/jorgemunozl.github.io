Working with infinite [[Vector Space]] we always assume that there exist the [[Basis Of A Space Vector (Hamel)]], but you need to prove it.

Now the demonstration of that claim, requires certain matters from the **Set Theory**, you have to define certain order relations [[Binary Relation]] and most important the [[Zermelo's Theorem]] which is (or maybe it was Zermelo's Lema) equivalent to the [[Axiom of Choice]].

You have to treat with the concepts of [[L.I Maximal Subset]] and you have it.

- The same question you have to do it when working with **infinite vectors spaces**. And ask (to improve your insight) why this demonstrations only works for finite **Vector Spaces**.
