$$
r_{\theta}\times r_{\phi}=-R(\sin \phi^{2}\cos \theta,\sin \phi^{2}\sin \theta,\sin \phi \cos \phi )

Then you take the length.

$$
\lVert r_{\theta}\times r_{\phi} \rVert =R^{2}\sin \phi
$$
