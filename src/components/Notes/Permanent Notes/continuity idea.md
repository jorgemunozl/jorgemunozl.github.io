_What does this mean?_

It's pretty obvious that this statement it's practically the same of the [[Limits (One Dimension)|limit]], you are evaluating the limit on $a$ , but exist one main difference, that is here you do touch the _center_ of the neighborhood, you do not restrict that point thus the function there is well defined and exist $f(a)$.

And like it's in essence a limit inherits the idea that for point nears the function evaluated in those points are also near.


>Being derivable, implies the continuity but not the other away around.
