This book introduce us to Alan Turing, one of the brilliant minds who open the world for computers.

After achieve performing tasks that are impossible to perform  humans with "computers", the natural question emerges, _Do machines have intelligence?_ This questions it's pretty if the answer if you say no why not trying to give intelligence to this machines, if you say yes you have to say define well the word _intelligence._

Turing particularly attempting to resolve this problem, he proposed another problem in form of a game [[Computing machinery and intelligence|The imitation game]].

The problems that are impossible to achieve with a traditional algorithms since you have to cover a infinite amount of possibilities and interestingly resolve this problems are the most important and beneficial for humans. ***Resolve intelligence and use it to resolve everything else***. _Hassabis_

>If we want to recognize a dog from a photo with a traditional algorithm we have to blown up our head, personally I tried do something similar but obviously I failed. [[computer vision]]!
