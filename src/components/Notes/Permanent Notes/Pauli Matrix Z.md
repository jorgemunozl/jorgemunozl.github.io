$$
\sigma_{z}=Z=\begin{bmatrix}
1&0 \\
0&-1
\end{bmatrix}
$$

Which [[EigenValues]] are $\pm 1$ and its [[Eigenvector]] are:
$$
\ket{z_{+}} = \ket{0} 
$$
$$
\ket{z_{-}} =\ket{1} 
$$
