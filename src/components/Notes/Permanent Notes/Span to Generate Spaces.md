Give it a [[Vector Space]]$V$ ,a subset  $G$, is a generator space if $span(G)=V$ [^1].
Where:
$$ span(G)=\left\{  v \in V|\exists\lambda_{i }\in F,v=\sum\lambda_{i}u_{i}, u_{i} \in G   \right\} $$

Let $B=\{ x_{1},x_{2},x_{3},\dots,x_{n} \}$ be a basis, then:
$$
\forall v \neq 0 \in V \exists \lambda_{1},\dots,\lambda_{n} \neq 0  | v=\lambda_{1}x_{1}+\dots \lambda_{n}x_{n}
$$
