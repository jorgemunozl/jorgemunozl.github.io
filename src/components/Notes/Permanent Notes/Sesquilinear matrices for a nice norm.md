Define [[Norm]] properly over the complex space.
$$
[u_{1},u_{2},u_{3}]*[u_{1},u_{2},u_{3}]^{\dagger}=[u_{1}\overline{u}_{1}+u_{2}\overline{u}_{2}+u_{3}\overline{u}_{3}]
$$
For that reason we define [[Sesquilinear form]].
