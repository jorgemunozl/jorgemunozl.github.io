> [!proposition]
>The [[Matrix representation of map|matrix form]] of the [[Adjoint Operator]] $A^{*}$ in any [[Orthonormal basis]] is the [[Dagger Operator, Conjugate Transpose Matrix|conjugate transpose matrix]] of $A$.

- A interesting way to watch it is via the [[Gram matrix]], there it is more clearly.
- Now if we take a [[Self Adjoint Operators|self adjoint operator]] then $A^{\dagger}=A$.  Whit $A^{\dagger}$ [[Dagger Operator, Conjugate Transpose Matrix|the conjugate transpose]], thus is easy see that the values on the diagonal should be **real values**.
