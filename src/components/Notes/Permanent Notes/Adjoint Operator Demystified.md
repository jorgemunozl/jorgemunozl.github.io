So looking at the [[Bra ket notation for make us the life easier]], we can obtain answers about the nature and behave of this **operator.**.

>[!proposition]
>Let $\ket{\psi}\bra{\phi}$ be an [[Linear Operator]], then the adjoint of that operator is:
>$$ \ket{\phi} \bra{\psi}   $$

Another fact is:

$$
\bra{v} \Omega ^{\dagger}\equiv \bra{\Omega v} 
$$

And:
$$ \bra{v} \Omega ^{\dagger}\ket{u} =\bra{u}\Omega \ket{v}^{*},\forall u,v   $$

Easy to get there form the definition, [[Adjoint Operator]].
Which is important for [[Quantum Mechanics MOC]].
