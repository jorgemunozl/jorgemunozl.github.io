The most known coordinate system is the _System Cartesian_ , here you use numbers to indicate the position of points.

In a two dimensional space we use two axis the $x$ and $y$, forming an angle of ninety degree The combination of this two give us all the points on this dimension using ordinate pairs $(x_{0},y_{0})$

In the three dimensional space we use three axis that form a 90 degree angle with each other, the $x$, $y$ and $z$. 

Of course this is the coordinate system more simpler, exist the [[Polar Coordinate System]], the [[Spherical and Cylindrical Coordinate System]], and more variants of these, for instance using parabolas.

In relativity exist a idea that is pretty related to the coordinate system.
