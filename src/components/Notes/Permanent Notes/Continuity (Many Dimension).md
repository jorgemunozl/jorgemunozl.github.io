Let $f$ be a [[Vector Function]]

Work equal but using norms, and set [[Open , closed balls and spheres]]
_Continuity_: The function is continuous on $t_{0}\in D$

$$
\forall \varepsilon >0,\ \exists  \delta>0, t\in D,\lvert t-t_{0} \rvert<\delta \implies\lVert f(t)-f(t_{0}) \rVert<\varepsilon 
$$


[[derivatives|Derivative]]:

$$
\lim_{ h\to 0 }\frac{1}{h}[f(x+h)-f(x)] 
$$
