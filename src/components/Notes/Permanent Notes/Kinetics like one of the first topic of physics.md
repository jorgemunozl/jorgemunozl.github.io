Kinetics is the branch of physics that study the movement of punctual objects. We don't care the causes of this, (for that we use [[Newton Second Law]]), here we assume that we have a defined _acceleration_. 

Now the first step is define a [[Reference Frame, and inertial frame|reference frame]], we can't simply say that the object is there. The most common is use the Cartesian coordinates because they are so practical, but there are some cases where is better use another systems (like the [[Polar Coordinate System]]).

Then you have the understand the concepts of _velocity_, _acceleration_ and _position_. We can interpret conceptually but is very well define using the language of [[derivatives]].

Also we can't forget the differentiate between media acceleration, velocity. (Zears Semansky)

Once you understand the movement in one dimension like free fall movement you pass to the [[Curved movement]].
