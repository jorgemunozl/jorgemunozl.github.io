So the famous $Y$ matrix written as:
$$
\sigma_{y}=\begin{bmatrix}
0&i \\
-i&0
\end{bmatrix}
$$
Now you care about the [[EigenValues]] are $\lambda=\pm 1$. And the [[Eigenvector]] are:

$$
\ket{y_{+}}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2 }}(\ket{0} + i\ket{1} ) 
$$
$$
\ket{y_{-}} =\frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2 }}(\ket{0} -i\ket{1} )
$$

Which represent the $Y$ axis on the [[Bloch Sphere, what it is|Bloch Sphere]]!
And interesting relation is: $HYH=-Y$ with $H$ the [[Hadamard Gate]].
