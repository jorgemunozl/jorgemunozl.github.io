>A **quadratic form** takes a vector and and return a number. Let $x$ be a column vector.
>$$
Q_{A}(x)=x^{T}Ax $$

- Where $A$ is [[Symmetric matrix]] of size $n\times n$.
- Is clearly that the result is based on quadratic terms of the vector $x$, (where the names comes from)                          
- We could say that practically a  matrix define for complete a quadratic form.
And we could define [[Definiteness of the matrix]] with this. A matrix is positive definite if:
$$
x^{T}Ax >0, \forall x\neq 0 $$
