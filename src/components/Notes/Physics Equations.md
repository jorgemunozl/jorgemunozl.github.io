Einstein Equation
$$G_{\mu \nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu \nu}=\kappa T_{\mu \nu} $$
Rotational Kinetic Energy
$$
K_{rot}=\frac{1}{2}I\omega^{2}
$$
Torque
$$
\vec{\tau}=\vec{r}\times \vec{F}=I\alpha
$$
Angular Momentum
$$
\vec{L}=I\omega
$$
Gravitational Potential Energy (two masses)
$$
U=-G \frac{m_{1}m_{2}}{r}
$$
First Law of Thermodynamics
Energy Conservation
$$
\Delta U=Q-V
$$
Work done during expansion/compression
$$
W=\int_{V_{i}}^{V_{f}}PdV
$$
$$
W=P\Delta V
$$
Isobaric:
$$
Q=nC_{P}\Delta T
$$
Isochoric:
$$
Q=nC_{V}\Delta T
$$
Isothermal (ideal gas):
$$
Q=W=nRT\ln\left( \frac{V_{f}}{V_{i}} \right)
$$
Specific Heats:
$$
C_{P}-C_{V}=R
$$
$$
PV=nRT
$$
Second Law of Thermodynamics
Entropy Change: 
$$
\Delta S=\int \frac{dQ_{\text{rev}}}{T}
$$
Entropy change for ideal gases (reversible):
$$
\Delta S=nC_{V}\ln\left( \frac{T_{f}}{T_{i}} \right)+nR\ln\left( \frac{V_{f}}{V_{i}} \right)
$$
