>[!definition]
>Let $f$ be a [[Scalar function]] $f(x,y,z)$ defined over $D$ such that the surface $S\subset D$. The surface integral is: [^2].
>$$\iint \limits_{S}f(x,y,z)dS=\iint_{D}f(\vec{r}(\mu,\nu))\lVert r_{\mu}\times r_{\nu} \rVert d\mu d\nu $$
- [[Surface on R3 via integrals]]
- If you compare it with the approach that we have with [[Line Integral (first specie)|line integral]] is pretty similar. 
- Think it like if you want to calculate the area of a fabric, and you have the equation of it, note that the area not depend if the fabric is very tall.Is kind of a weight sum
