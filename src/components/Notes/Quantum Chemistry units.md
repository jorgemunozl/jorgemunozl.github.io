[[Quantum Numbers]] also called **atomic units**. (a.u) Hartree atomic units

Natural system 

**Distance**: So the Bohr radius is :
$$ a_{0}=\frac{4\pi\varepsilon_{0}\hbar^{2}}{m_{e}e^{2}}\approx 0.529 \overset{\circ}{\text{A}} $$
Where $\overset{\circ}{\text{A}}=10^{-10}$ is called **Armstrong**.

**Energy**: Hartree energy $E_{h}$

$$
E_{h}=\frac{e^{2}}{4\pi\varepsilon_{0}a_{0}}
$$

**Time**:

$$
t_{0}=\frac{\hbar}{E_{h}}
$$

**Velocity**:

$$
v_{0}=\frac{a_{0}}{t_{0}} \approx 2.19\times 10^{6}m /s
$$
