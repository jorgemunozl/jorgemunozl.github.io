>[!theorem]
>Let $A\in \mathbb{R}^{m\times n}$, the **SVD** is a descomposition of the form.[^1]
>$$ A=U\Sigma V^{\mathsf{\top}} $$
>Where $U\in \mathbb{R}^{m\times m}$ an [[Orthogonal operator]] with column vectors, $V\in \mathbb{R}^{n\times n}$, and $\Sigma$ a [[Diagonalizable Matrix]].

- Interesting matter happens when you use it along the definition of [[Cofactor Matrix]]
- What happens with [[Singular Matrix]]

[^1]:  [[Mathematics for Machine Learning]] pp. 125
