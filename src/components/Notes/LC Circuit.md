In this case you only have a [[Capacitors]] and an [[Inductors]]. Using the [[Kirchhoff Law]] you obtain that.

$$
\frac{Q}{C}-L\frac{d}{dt} I=0
$$

Then it's clear that this is a [[Simple Harmonic Motion]]. Where the [[Angular velocity]] $\omega_{0}$ is equal to:

$$
\omega_{0}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{ LC }}
$$

And the solution is simply, (from the initial conditions).
$$
Q(t)=Q_{0}\cos(\omega_{0}t)
$$
The charges come and back a lot of times.

From this we go to the [[RL Circuits]]
