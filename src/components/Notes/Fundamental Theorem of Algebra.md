>[!theorem]
>Every [[Polynomial]] over $\mathbb{C}$ has at least one root. 


And the prove it is done using all minus pure algebra we need to appeal to _analysis_.


$p\in P(\mathbb{C})$ no constant, $p$ has unique factorization (salvo el orden de los factores)

$$
p(z)=C(z-\lambda_{1})\dots(z-\lambda_{m})
$$

$c,\lambda_{1},\dots,\lambda_{m}\in \mathbb{C}$

Theorem. $p \in P(\mathbb{C})$ has all its coefficients real. Si $\lambda \in \mathbb{C}$ is root of $p$ then $\overline{\lambda}$ also is a root of $p$.
