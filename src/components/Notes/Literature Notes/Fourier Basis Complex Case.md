For function defined on $[0,2\pi]$, the classical Fourier orthogonal basis consist on:
$$
\{ e^{ inx } \}_{n\in \mathbb{Z} }
$$

The satisfy the orthogonality condition:

$$
\int_{0}^{2\pi}e^{ inx }e^{ -imx}dx=2\pi \delta _{mm}
$$

Where $\delta$ is the [[Kronecker's Delta]].
