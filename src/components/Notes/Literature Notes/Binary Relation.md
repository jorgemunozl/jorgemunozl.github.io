>[!Definition] 
>A **binary relation** $R$ in a set $S$, is a **subset** of the Cartesian product $S\times S$. 
>If in a some way $a$ is related to $b$ ($a,b \in S$) then the pair $(a,b) \in R$ [^1].

1.  $R$ is **reflexive** if: $\forall x\in S,(x,x)\in R$.
2. $R$ is **symmetric** if: $(x,y)\in R\implies(y,x)\in R$
3. $R$ is **transitive** if:  $(x,y)\in R\land(y,z)\in R\implies(x,z)\in R$
4. $R$ is **anti-symmetry** if: $(x,y)\in R\land (y,x)\in R\implies x=y$
