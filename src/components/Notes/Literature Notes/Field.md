>[!definition]
>A **field** is an [[Algebraic Structure]], mostly denote by a set $F$ equipped with two operations , addition and multiplication. $(F,+,\cdot)$. [^1]

- This follow a list of classic axioms, but instead we can simply say that it's a _unital_ [[Ring]] _with division_. 
- A field $F$ is called _algebraically closed_ is every [[Polynomial]] with coefficients in $F$ has a root on $F$. 
- A example is $\mathbb{C}$, very related to [[Fundamental Theorem of Algebra]].
- Mention that also here exist [[Homo and isomorphism-Group|Homo and isomorphism]] but do it the same is repetitive.


[^1]: [[Linear Algebra Springer]] pp. 39
