>[!definition]
One function $p:\mathbb{K}\to \mathbb{K}$ is called a **_polynomial_** with coefficient on $\mathbb{K}$ if exist $m\in \mathbb{N}_{0}$, $\{ a_{m} \}\in \mathbb{K}$ such that:
>$$
\forall z \in \mathbb{K}:p(z)=a_{0}+a_{1}z+a_{2}z^{2}+\dots+a_{m}z^{m}
>$$
