This is famous inequality that rises the notion of angle in more complex spaces than the Euclid one.

>[!proposition]
>Give it two vectors $u,v$, a [[Inner product]] then [^1].
>$$ \frac{\langle u,v \rangle }{\lVert u \rVert \lVert v \rVert }\leq 1 $$

- Now is clear that there we are gonna to assign an angle, but for what reason not assign the $\sin$.
- Important for [[Steepest Descent]].

[^1]: [[Linear Algebra Done Right]] pp. 189
