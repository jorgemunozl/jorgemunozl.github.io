When the movement is don't give it in one dimension we talk of curved movement.

Here we talk of _parabolic motion_ and the ones what are ruled by functions.

To facilitate our analysis is common used another [[Reference Frame, and inertial frame]], the most know is the [[Polar Coordinate System]].

This is, give it the _vector position norm_ and a expression of the angle between this and the $x$ axis. We could obtain deriving $\vec{r}=\mathbf{r}\hat{u}_{r}$, and using the relation between ,$\hat{u}_{r}$ and $\hat{u}_{\theta}$.

$$
\vec{v}=\mathbf{v_{r}}\hat{u_{r}}+\mathbf{v_{\theta}}\hat{u_{\theta}}$$

Where $\mathbf{v_{r}}=\frac{dr}{dt},\mathbf{v_{\theta}}=r\frac{d\theta}{dt}$, we can make the same for the _acceleration_. 

One important type of movement is the [[Circular Motion]]
