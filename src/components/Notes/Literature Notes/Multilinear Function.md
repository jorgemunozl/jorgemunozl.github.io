>[!definition]
>A **multilinear function** $T$ is a [[Map like the generalization of a function]]:
> $$
T:V_{1}\times V_{2}\times \dots \times V_{k}\to \mathbb{F}$$
>Where $V_{1},V_{2},\dots ,V_{k}$ are [[Vector Space]] over the same [[Field]] $\mathbb{F}$. [^1]

 - The case were the $T:V\times V\to \mathbb{F}$ is called a _bilinear function_ (or maybe functional) or _bilinear form_. [[Sesquilinear form]],And in some cases you called it [[Inner product]].
- Now the set of all the multilinear functions it like a generalized [[Dual Space]].
- [^2] called **functional**.
