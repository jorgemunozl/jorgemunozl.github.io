>[!definition]
>The **algebraic** **multiplicity** of the [[EigenValues|eigenvalue]] $\lambda$ of the matrix $A$ is the maximum natural number $\mu$ such that:
$$(\lambda-x)^{\mu} \text{ is factor of } P_{A}(x)$$
>Where $P_{A}(x)$ is the [[Characteristic Polynomial]].

>[!note] Numbers of EigenValues of A counting multiplicities.
>We define $\mu$ like the sum of all the algebraic multiplicity, this is:
>$\mu=\sum_{i=1}\mu_{i}$
>It's obvious that $\mu\leq n, n=dim(V)$
