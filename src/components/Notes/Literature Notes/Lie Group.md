[[General Linear Group]]

>[!tip] Definition
>A lie group $(G,\cdot)$ is a group, that also is a smoth variety-manifold, and satifies the follow:
> 1. The operation of the group $\cdot :G\times G \to G$, defined by $(g,h)$ $\mapsto g\cdot h$ is a smooth application. 
> 2. The inversion   $^{-1}: G\times G \to G,$ defined by $g \mapsto g^{-1}$ is smooth map.
>
