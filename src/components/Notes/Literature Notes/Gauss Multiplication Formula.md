A beautiful relation prop is:

>[!proposition]
>$$\prod_{k=0}^{n-1}\Gamma(z+\frac{k}{n})=(2\pi)^{(n-1)/2}n^{1/2-nz}\Gamma (nz)$$

- The prove relies on the [[Weierstrass's Definition of Function Gamma]], and some fancy tricks that I am not dispose to use.
- I think that is a clean way to look at the [[Gamma Function]] of fractions that is impossible for us to at least calculate, for rationals.

Now why we would care about it? Nothing special the truth.
