This a pretty theorem which relates [[Dimension of a Vector space]], [[Kernel of Linear Map]] and [[Range of a Linear Map]] of a [[Vector Space]] $V$. [^1]

>[!theorem]
>$$dim_{\mathbb{K}}(Ran(\varphi))+dim_{\mathbb{K}}(Ker(\varphi))=dim_{\mathbb{K}}(V)$$
>With $\varphi$ a [[Linear Map]]

- This tell that the Range and the ker in some way define the size of the space via a linear map.
- Also called the **Fundamental Theorem of Linear Maps**
- And here the name **Rank** comes from another definitions.


[^1]: [[Linear Algebra Done Right]] pp. 62
