The Kernel-nucleus-null space of a [[Linear Map]]  it's a _set of vectors_ that belongs to _starting set_ such that applying said transformation the result it's zero. This is:

Let $T:V\to W$ be a linear transformation, then its nucleus is: [^1]
$$
ker(T)=\{ v \in V | T(v)= 0 \}
$$
- Think of the elements like doing nothing interesting, they are quite bored and never return something that we actually care, the zero.

[^1]: [[Linear Algebra Done Right]] pp. 73
