>[!definition] 
>Let $z$ be a [[Complex Numbers]] such that $\mathfrak{R}(z)> 0$. The function gamma of $z$ is: [^1]
$$\begin{align}\Gamma(z)=\int_{0}^{\infty} u^{z-1}e^{-u}du\end{align}$$

- The gamma function is a function that extends the concepts of [[Factorial|factorial]] including to the positive real numbers.
- It can be represented both in its _integral form_ and as a [[Euler's Definition as an Infinite Product of the gamma function|limit-based]] that lead to a infinite product.
- If we chose a $z \in \mathbb{N}$  the **gamma** **function** takes the [[Factorial]] form: $\Gamma(z)= (z-1)!$
