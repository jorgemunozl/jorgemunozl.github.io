A function is the consequence of relate two sets $A$ and $B$, $f:A\to B$, in such a way that to each element of $A$ (_domain_) correspond a unique element of $B$ (_co domain_). [^1]

More precisely we could say that a **function is a subset** of $A\times B$, this is ordered pairs. 

We are talking in the more possible sense $A$ could be the set of friends that you have and $B$ a integer number. We need to let go this idea that the _input and output_ are numbers, it's important to clarify that we can give it anything we want such as _triangles_, _matrix_, _polynomials_, _sets_, even another _functions_. (This is known like a [[Functional]]), but in this cases we talk more about [[Map like the generalization of a function|maps]]. 

The way of how this are converted is not a huge deal, the well known box, that receive an _input_ and return and _output_. And you do not care what happens inside the box. (**Black box**).

The most know, studied and useful are [[Real function|real functions]], that we learn in secondary.

>To Riemann a function is essentially a deformation that is applied to a surface or a curve.
