- This can see viewed in two cases [^1] **multiplication and quotient**, the **sandwich case which is trivial**.

>[!definition]
>Let $a$ and $b$ be two [[Sequence]]. We look at the **quotient**.
>$$ L=\lim_{ n \to \infty } \frac{a_{n}}{b_{n}} $$
>If $L$ is a real number then both have the same behavior.
>If $L=0$ and $\sum b_{n}$ converges , then $\sum a_{n}$ also **converges**.
>If $L=\infty$ and $\sum b_n$ diverges, then the other also **diverges**.

- The another cases are **incloncusive**. 
- But this need to have information about two sequences, not just one, but you can always create another one.
- We could think it like a way of compare two series, if I have information about the first I could say something about the second,(they are [[Entanglement|entangled]]). 
- Now the beauty relies on how it is related with the [[Root Cauchy Criterion]], because this could be like a specific case.

[^1]: [[Analisis Real Elon Lages]] pp. 57
