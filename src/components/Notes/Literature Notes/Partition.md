>[!definition]
> A partition of $X$ is a family $\mathcal{P}=\{ P_{i}:i \in I \}$ of non empty subsets of $X$,  in such a way that its union is $X$ and all the subsets are disjoint each to other. 

>$(I \text{ set of indices}, P \text{ a familia indicated family of subsets})$
