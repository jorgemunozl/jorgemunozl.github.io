Give it two [[Series]] in $\mathbb{R}$,$a_{i},b_{i}$. Their _Cauchy Product_ is another serie, $e_{i}$ in such a way that $e_{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}a_{k}b_{n-k}$, for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$.

>[!tip] Definition

Now observe that in any moment we mention the divergence or convergence of the series.

Now this is pretty useful because you can use the [[uniqueness of power series]]
