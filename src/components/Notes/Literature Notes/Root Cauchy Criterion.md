>[!criterion]
>Let $a_{n}$ be a [[Sequence]], if: (for all $n$)
>$$\lvert a_{n}^{\frac{1}{n} }\vert\leq c<1$$
>The the [[Series]] $\sum a_{n}$ [[Absolutely Convergent Series]].

- Is clear (like in the another case) that if the root is greater than one the series **diverges**. [^1]
- Important to mention that [[D'Alembert Criterion]] is stronger than this criterion.

[^1]: [[Analisis Real Elon Lages]] pp. 58
