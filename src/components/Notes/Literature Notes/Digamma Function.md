>[!definition] 
>The **digamma** **function** is defined as the logarithmic derivative of the [[Gamma Function]]. 
$$\begin{align}\psi(z)=\frac{d}{dz} ln(\Gamma(z))\end{align}$$
Where $z$ is a complex number.  




It's very interesting to see that the function evaluated at one is equal to the negative of [[Euler-Mascheroni Constant]]
$$
\psi(1)=-\gamma
$$
All the mathematic is connected is really beautiful.
