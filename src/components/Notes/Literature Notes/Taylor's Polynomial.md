Let $f$ be a infinite differentiable [[Real function]] then:
 
$$
f(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{f^{(n)}(a)}{n!}(x-a)^{n} 
$$

If $f$ has nth derivative we could a form with [residue](PolynomioTProve.png). 
 
If the function don't have infinite derivatives we denotate symbol sum limits to reach the last different zero function and appears a _residue_.
