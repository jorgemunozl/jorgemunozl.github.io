>[!definition]
>Let $f$ be a complex Function then its [[Fourier]] transform is[^1] :
 >$$ \mathcal{F}[f(t)](z)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(t)e^{-izt }  \, dt  $$

- Is a **complex function** so we can't visualize it. 
- Is clear that is inspired on the [[Fourier Series]] via the [[Euler's Formula]].

And the inverse transform is:
$$
f(k)=\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} F(k)e^{ ik } \, dx 
$$
$f,g\in L^{1}(\mathbb{R})$
$$
\mathcal{F}[f](\xi)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(x)e^{ -2\pi ix \xi}  \, dx 
$$
