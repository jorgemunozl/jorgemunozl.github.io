>[!definition] 
>If every **Cauchy sequence** of points in the metric space has a limit (**converges**) and this limit belong to the metric space then the metric space is called **complete**. [[Cauchy's Criterion For Sequences|Cauchy Sequences]] [^1]

>[!note]
>Let $(X,d)$ be a [[Metric Space]] a sequence of elements of $X$ is called **Cauchy** if for every positive real number $r>0$ there is a positive integer $N$ such that for all positive integer $m,n$, $d(x_{m},x_{n})<r$.

- The most important property of this is its spaces is that there no exist **gaps**.
- The most important for us is ask: The [[Hilbert Space]] is complete? And why that matters?
