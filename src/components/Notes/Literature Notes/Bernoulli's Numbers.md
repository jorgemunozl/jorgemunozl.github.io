>[!definition]
>The n-th **Bernoulli** **number** is the independent coefficient of the n-th [[Bernoulli Polynomial]] [^1]. This is:
>$$B_{n}=B_{n}(0)$$

- It's possible to demonstrate that the __nth Bernoulli bigger or equal than two number__ could be expressed like:
$$
B_{n}=(B+1)^{n}
$$
- The first Bernoulli number is for applying the definition and the second you could obtained recursively.
