We call **eigen vector** to a vector where the [[EigenValues|eigenvalue equation]] is satisfied because all the vectors could satisfy the eigenvalue equation, just a set of all the vector can satisfy it [^1].

- When we refer to _eigenvector_ we refer to a whole family of vectors that are "multiple", because only they differentiate by one scalar.
- Once we have the _eigenvalues_ we obtain the eigenvector $v$ solving: $(A-I\lambda)v=0$, you give them arbitrary parameters to $v$ and solve.
- They are  [[Linearly independent]], which is intuitive to think, so another question that arise is how much of them can we have? And the unique number that would have sense is the [[Dimension of a Vector space|dimension]]. Yeah the maxima number of them is that number!
