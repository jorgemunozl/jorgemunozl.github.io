>[!definition]
Let $f$ be a [[Generalized eigenvector]] of $A$ with [[EigenValues]] $\lambda$, let $p \in \mathbb{N}$ the lower number such that $(A-\lambda id_{v})^{p}(f)=0_{V}$. The ordered p-tuple.
>$$
((A-\lambda id_{v})^{p-1}(f),(A-\lambda id_{v})^{p-2}(f),\dots(A-\lambda id_{v})(f),f)
>$$
is called **_generalized** **eigenvector** **cycle_** generated by $f$ and correspondent to $\lambda \in S$, the length is $p$.
