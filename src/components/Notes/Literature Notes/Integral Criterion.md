>[!criterion]
>Let $f$ be a **decreasing** function positive continuous.
>- If the **integral converges** the [[Series]] also **converges**.
>- If the **integral diverges**, then the [[Series]] also **diverges**.

- Another relation between **integrals** and its series, the other would be. [[Euler McLaurin's Formula]].  
- Important to mention [^1] that you evaluate the integral on the domain $[1,\infty>$

[^1]: [[Criterios de convergencia en infinitas sumatorias]]
