An $L^{2}$ space is a space where the integral of the squared absolute value of the function is finite (well defined).

For a function defined on an interval $[a,b]$, the $L^{2}$-norm is:

$$
\lVert f \rVert_{2}=\left( \int _{a}^{b}\lvert f(x) \rvert ^{2} \right)^{1/2} 
$$

The important of this space is that has an [[Inner product]] defined by:

$$
\langle f,g \rangle=\int_{a}^{b}f(x)\overline{g(x)}dx 
$$

That for us is kind of beautiful because [[Fourier]].
