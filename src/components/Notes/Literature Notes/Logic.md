### Logic from a math perspective. (Formal)

Is the skill of systematically manipulate propositions. We play with demonstrations, phalacies, paradoxes, searching the notion of true.

Here you create or is given some __propositions__ we do not care if these propositions have sense or not, here we focus on from these statement reach to useful results. Of course the origin of this __statements__ are uniquely product of the human intelligence and is interesting search how this are created or thought.
_Mathematically talking this statements are [[Math Axioms]]_.
