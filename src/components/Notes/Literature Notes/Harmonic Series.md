The [[Series]]:
$$H_{n}=\sum_{i=1}^{N} \frac{1}{n}$$
Is called **Harmonic Series**.
- And you can prove when $N$ tends to infinite the series **diverges**.
- [[Euler-Mascheroni Constant]] is the best approximation for this **Series**.
