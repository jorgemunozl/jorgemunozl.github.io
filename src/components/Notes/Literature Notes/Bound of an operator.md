>[!definition]
>1. Let $\mathcal{N_{1}}$ and $\mathcal{N}_{2}$ norm spaces. The [[Linear Map]] $T:\mathcal{N}_{1}\to \mathcal{N}_{2}$ is **bounded** if:
>$$
\exists c \in \mathbb{R}^{+}: \left\lVert  T\left( \frac{f}{\lVert f \rVert }_{1} \right)  \right\rVert _{2}\leq c
>$$
2. Let $\mathcal{N}$ be a norm space, $A$ [[Linear Operator]] on $\mathcal{N}$. The **bound** of $A$ is:
>$$
\lVert A \rVert=sup \left\{  \frac{\lVert U(f) \rVert }{\lVert f \rVert }\in \mathbb{R}_{0}^{+}|f\neq 0_{\mathcal{N}}\in \mathcal{N}   \right\} 
>$$
