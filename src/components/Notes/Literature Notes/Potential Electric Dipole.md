Considering $d\ll r$.

$$ V=V_{1}+V_{2}=kq\left( \frac{1}{r_{1}}-\frac{1}{r_{2}} \right) $$
With the fact that [[Electric dipole]] $\rho=dq$ and 
And using a matter of cosines:

We see that:
$$ \mathbf{r_{1}}=\mathbf{\frac{d}{2}}+\mathbf{r} $$
$$ \mathbf{r_{2}}=\mathbf{\frac{d}{2}}-\mathbf{r} $$

Taking norms and applying the binomial expansion:

$$r_{1}=r+\frac{d^{2}}{8r}-\frac{d}{2}\cos(\theta)$$

And here comes another [[binomial expansion]]. And the fact $r_{i}\approx r$ thus:

$$\frac{1}{r_{i}}=\frac{1}{r}\left( 1\pm \frac{d}{2r}\cos (\theta) \right)$$

And you got it:
$$ V=kp\frac{\cos\theta}{r^{2}} =k\frac{\vec{\rho}\cdot \vec{r}}{r^{3}}$$
