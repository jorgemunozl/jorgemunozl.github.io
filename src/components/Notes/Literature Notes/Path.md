>[!definition]
>Give it a [[Vector function over a real variable|function]] $f:\mathbb{R}\to \mathbb{R}^{n}$, we say that is a **path** or **curve** if is [^1] [[Continuity (Many Dimension)|continuous]]. And even is called **trajectory in other references**.[^2]

- Exist some interesting definitions around this. We could define: **Closed path (final and start equal), simple (inyective).**
- Some references call **curve** to the [[Trace and graph]].
- We talk of regular curve, when the curve is differentiable over its domain.
- Also we could use this idea and extended to [[path in topological spaces|topological spaces]].

[^1]: [[Multivariable Calculus Lectures]] pp. 59

[^2]: [[Analisis Matematico III Calculo Vectorial]] pp.133
