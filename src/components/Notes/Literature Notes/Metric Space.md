>[!definition]
>A metric $d$ is a [[Binary Operation]] defined over a set $X$, $d:(X,X)\to \mathbb{R}$ that que satisfy the follow axioms:
>- Symmetry
>- Triangular Inequality
>- Positivity
>- Identical Property
>To the tuple ($X,d$) we call a __metric space__.

- If we want to generalize the concept of distance, that we already know well in the numbers reals, we have to study to respective theory of Metric Space
- With these axioms we call reflect well the sense of __distance__. And more important generalize and expand all its subtopic using the well know continuity of real numbers.
