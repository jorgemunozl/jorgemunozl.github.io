The orthogonal space is $\mathrm{R}^{\perp}$ to $R$ is the set: $R$ is any **set**. [^1]
$$ R ^{\perp}:=\{  f\in \mathcal{E}|\forall g \in R:(f,g)=0 \} $$
- $R ^{\perp}$ is [[Sub Vector Space]]
- $R\equiv(R ^{\perp})^{\perp}$

- We say that $R ^{ \perp}$ is the **orthogonal complement** of $R$. When $R$ [[Sub Vector Space]], $\mathcal{E}=R\oplus R ^{\perp}$. The thing about dimension get obvious.
