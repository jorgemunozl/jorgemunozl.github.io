A concept that it is pretty related to [[field lines]], when the leaf it is perpendicular we have the simple case that is:
$$
\Phi_{E}=EA
$$

But we need to generalize it like this: (for a little part of area $i$)
$$
\Phi_{E,i}=\vec{E_{i}}\cdot\Delta \vec{A}_{i}
$$

Summing and taking the limit.

$$
\Phi_{E}\equiv \int \vec{E}\cdot d \vec{A}
$$

Talking in a closed surface, we need to use the correct notation:

$$
\Phi_{E}=\oint \vec{E}\cdot d\vec{A}
$$

This give rise to the [[Gauss Law]]
