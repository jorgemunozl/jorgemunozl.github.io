>[!restriction]
>If we restrict the the concept of [[Accumulation Points - Metric Space|acumulation points on metrics spaces]] to the [[Real Numbers]], and with the [[Euclid Norm, product| the regular norm]] we have:
- Let $p\in A$ is _accumulation point_ of $A\subset \mathbb{R}$ if: [^1]
$$\forall \epsilon>0,\exists x \in A-\{ p \} :  \ \ \lvert x-p \rvert < \epsilon $$

Another way to said it:

- Let $a\in \mathbb{R}$, the _neighborhood_ with radius delta $\delta$ is the set:
$$
\nu_{\delta}(a)= <a-\delta,a+\delta\> > = \{ x \in \mathbb{R} | a-\delta <x<a+\delta\}
$$
- $a$ is accumulation point of $D$ _non-empty set_ of for all neighborhood contain at least one point of the $D$ set different of $a$. This is:
$$
(\nu_{\delta}(a)-\{ a \})\cap D\neq \emptyset ,\forall \delta>0
$$
