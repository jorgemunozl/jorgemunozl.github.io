A neighborhood system assigns to each point $x\in X$ a collection $\mathcal{N}(x)$ of subsets of $X$, to this subset we called _neighborhoods_ of $x$, which must satisfy:
1. Each nieghborhood of $x$ contains $x$.
2. If $N$ is a neighborhood of $x$, then there exist another neighborhood $M$ of $x$ such that $M\subseteq N$ (i.e., neighborhoods form a local structure around $x$).
3. The intersection of two neighborhoods of $x$ is a also a neighborhood of $x$
4. If $N$ is a neighborhood of $x$ and $N$ contains a set $U$ such that $x\in U$, then $U$ is also a neighborhood of $x$.
