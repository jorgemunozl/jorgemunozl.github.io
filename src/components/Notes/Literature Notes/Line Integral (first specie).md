>[!definition]
>Let $\vec{\alpha}:[a,b]\to \mathbb{R}^{n}$ ([[Vector function over a real variable|function]]) be , a [[Path|regular curve]] such that $\vec{\alpha}([a,b])=\mathcal{C}$.
>Let $f:\mathbb{R}^{n}\to \mathbb{R}$ ([[Scalar function]]) be a _integrable function_ over $U$ such that $\mathcal{C} \subset U$.
>We call **line integral (of first specie)** of $f$ over $\mathcal{C}$ to the integral [^1].
>$$
\int \limits_{\mathcal{C}}fds=\int _{a}^{b}f(\alpha(t))   \lVert  \alpha'(t)\rVert   dt $$
- Also know as the **scalar line integral**.
