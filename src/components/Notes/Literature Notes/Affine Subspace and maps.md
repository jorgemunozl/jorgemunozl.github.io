>[!definition]
>Let $V$ be a [[Vector Space]], $U$ a [[Sub Vector Space]] of $V$, $x \in V$. 
>A affine space $L$ is the set:
>$$L=\{ x + v | v \in U\}$$
>Let $W$ be another **Vector space**.
>Let $\phi:V\to W$ a [[Linear Map]], and $a\in W$ then.
>$$\psi:V\to W, x\to a+\phi(x)$$
>It's a **affine map** [^1].

- Now is usual use the notation $v+W$, sum of a vector with a space, now Armonicos have an interesting about this.
- If you look closely is pretty similar to the the idea of [[coset about the quotient space]].

[^1]: [[Mathematics for Machine Learning]] pp. 61
