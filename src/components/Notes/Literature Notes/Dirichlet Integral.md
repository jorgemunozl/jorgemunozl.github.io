The integral with the name of Peter Dirichlet is:
$$
I=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{\sin(x)}{x} \, dx 
$$
Using the _Feynman Technique_ adding $e^{ -sx }$ its easy to prove that $I=\pi$.

Now if you want to practice only the _Feynman Technique_ it's right. 

But it's a good example to show something interesting. What if we sustitude $x$ by $nx$. The question is if you reach a specific $n$ the pattern that you would discpver break out. And the beautiful is that the explanation of that fenomena is passing the 
[[Fourier Transform]] of the function. Now many questions appears one of this is hpw you make the FT of a no periodic function.
