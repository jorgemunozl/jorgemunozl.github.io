Let $f$ be a [[Scalar function]] We could approximate to $f$ using the [[Gradient]] and the [[Hessian matrix]].

When $\mathrm{h}$ is small (the norm):
$$
f(\mathrm{x+h})=f(\mathrm{x})+\nabla f(\mathrm{x})\cdot \mathrm{h}+\mathrm{h}H_{f}(\mathrm{x})\mathrm{h}^{T}
$$

Now you prove this? How you prove [[Taylor's Polynomial]]?
