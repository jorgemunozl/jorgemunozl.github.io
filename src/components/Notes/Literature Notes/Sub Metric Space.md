>[!definition]
>Let $(X,d)$ be a [[Metric Space]], $A\subset X$ a set. The **metric** **space** $(A,d_{A})$ with:
>$$
\begin{align}
d_{A}:A&\to \mathbb{R} \\
a &\mapsto d_{A}(a)=d(a)
\end{align}
>$$
>is called sub metric space of $(X,d)$.

- If you restrict the metric and only use the subset you have a sub metric space.
