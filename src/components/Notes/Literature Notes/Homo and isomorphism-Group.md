>[!definition]
Let $(G,g)$ and $(H,h)$ be two [[Group|groups]], a **homomorphism** is a [[Map like the generalization of a function|map]] $\varphi$ in such a way. [^1]
>$$
\varphi(g(a,b))=h(\varphi(a),\varphi(b)) $$

- If $\varphi$ is [[Bijective]] then we say that the map is a **isomorphism** and the two groups are isomorphic. But why we would use that name?
- [[Preserve the structure - Homomorphism meaning]], they are equal under the lens of the action!
