A homomorphism ($\varphi$) is a [[Map like the generalization of a function|map]] between two [[Vector Space]] over the same [[Field]], in such a way that the next it's true. 

>[!hint] Definition
>
>$$\begin{align} \varphi &:V \rightarrow W \\
>\varphi(a+_{v}b)&=\varphi(a)+_{w}\varphi(b) \\
  \varphi(\alpha \cdot_{v}f)&=\alpha \cdot_{w} \varphi(f)
\end{align}$$
- This is the same definition of [[Linear Map]].
