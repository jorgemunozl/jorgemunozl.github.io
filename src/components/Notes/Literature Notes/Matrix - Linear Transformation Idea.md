Once the concept of a [[Linear Map]] is introduced, we may ask whether it can be represented by a matrix (i.e., multiplying coefficients as in matrix multiplication ,concept intuitive of linearity). 
The answer is yes: there is a bijection between the space of matrices and the space of linear maps. 
Due to the abstract nature of vectors (which depend on the vector space), we use [[Coordinates Linear Algebra]] in a ordered [[Basis Of A Space Vector (Hamel)|Basis]] to represent them.
