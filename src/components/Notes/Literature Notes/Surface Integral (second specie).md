>[!definition]
>Let $S$ be a regular surface parameterized, a [[Vector field]] $\vec{F}=(F_{1},F_{2},F_{3})$ define over $U$, such that $S\subset U$ we call **surface integral** (also flux of $\vec{F}$ over $S$) to [^1] :
>$$ \iint_{S}\vec{F}\cdot d\mathbf{\vec{S}}=\iint \vec{F}(\vec{r}(\mu,\nu))\cdot(\vec{r}_{\mu}\times \vec{r}_{\nu})d\mu d\nu $$
Where $d\mathbf{\vec{S}}=\vec{n}dS$ , $\vec{n}$ is a _normal vector_ that point outwards to the convex, further explain.
- Also called flux of $\vec{F}$ over $S$ 
- Measure how the vector field flows through the surface.
