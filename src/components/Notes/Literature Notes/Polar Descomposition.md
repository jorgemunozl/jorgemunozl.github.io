>[!proposition]
>Every matrix in the [[Euclid Vector Space]] with [[Determinant of a matrix|determinant]] different from **zero** could be written as the [[composition]] of a [[Orthogonal operator]] and a [[Self Adjoint Operators|self adjoint operator]]: [^1] [^2]
>$$ S = A\circ O $$
- With $A$ **self adjoint** and $O$ **orthogonal**.
- Stretch and rotations.
- They are [[Singular Value Descomposition]] under the hood!

 >It's a beauty say: It's a generalization of [[Complex number angle and radius]].

[^1]: [[Linear Algebra Springer]] pp. 296
[^2]: [[Linear Algebra and Geometry Manim]] pp. 158
