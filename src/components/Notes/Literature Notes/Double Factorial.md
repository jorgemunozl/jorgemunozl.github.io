>[!definition]
For a $n\in \mathbb{N}$ **even** the double [[Factorial]] of $n$: [^1]
>$$ n!!=n(n-2)(n-4)\dots2 >$$
>For $n$ **_odd_**:
>$$ n!! = n(n-2)\dots 3.1  $$

This is:
$$ (2k)!!=2^{k}( k!)$$
And
$$ (2k-1)!!=\frac{(2k)!}{k!2^{k}}=\frac{(2k)!}{(2k)!!} $$

$$ (2k)!!(2k-1)!!=(2k)! $$
