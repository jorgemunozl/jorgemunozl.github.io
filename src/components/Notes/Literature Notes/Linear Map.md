>[!definition]
>Give two [[Vector Space]] $V,W$, the linear map $T:V\to W$ is linear if the following is true[^1].
>The ___homogeneity:___ 
>$$
T(\lambda x)=\lambda T(x) $$
>And the ***additivity:***
>$$ T(x+y)=T(x)+T(y) $$

- Is about sum and scalars, always was that. [[Being linear, belong to the same space after operations]].
- A linear [[Map like the generalization of a function|map]] by satisfying those two properties we can demonstrate a ton of theorems.

[^1]: [[Linear Algebra Done Right]] pp. 52
