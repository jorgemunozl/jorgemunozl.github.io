>[!definition]
Give a [[Vector Space]] V,a set $M \subset V$, we say that the _vector space_ that $M$ generates with the  restriction sum and the restriction product and the field is a **_sub** **vector** **space_** of the first.

- It's easy to see (**Prop**) that every subset of $V$ generates a _sub vector space_ if:
$$
\begin{align}
1.& 0_{\mathbf{v}} \in M \\
2.& \forall f,g \in M, \forall \lambda \in \mathbb{K}, f+\lambda g \in M
\end{align}
$$
