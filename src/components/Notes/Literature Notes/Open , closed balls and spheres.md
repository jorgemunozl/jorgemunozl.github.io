>[!definition]
Give it a [[Metric Space]] $(X,d)$, $x\in X,r\in \mathbb{R}$, the set $B(x,r)$ is a **open** **ball**:
>$$B(x,r)=\{ y\in X |d(x,y)<r\}$$ $\overline{B}(x,r)$ is a _close ball_:
>$$
B(x,r)=\{ y\in X |d(x,y)\leq r\}$$
$S(x,r)$ is a _sphere_:
>$$B(x,r)=\{ y\in X |d(x,y)=r\}$$

- The __open ball__ ($B$) do not include the "border" ($<$), in the other hand the __closed__ one ($\hat{B}$) does ($\leq$), of course you have to define a _point of center_ and a real number like radius.
- Also we have the __spheres__ ($S(x,r)$) (is the border (=)).
- This are very to important when one is [[Balls to study of sets 1|study sets on a metric space]].
