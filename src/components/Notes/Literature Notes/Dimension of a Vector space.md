>[!definition]
>Give it a [[Vector Space]] its **dimension** is the **supreme** of the cardinal of the set of cardinals of all the [[Linearly independent]] set, this is:
$$D_{V,\mathbb{K}}=\{ n\in \mathbb{N} :card(R)=n \land R \text{ l.i}\}$$

- Or also is valid we say is the cardinal of the any [[Basis Of A Space Vector (Hamel)|basis]]. (Corollary of the definition) 
- The dimension also depends on the field.
- It's obvious that: $dim(M)\leq dim(V)$, where $M$ represent a [[Sub Vector Space]]. (monotony of dimension) Prove it.
-If $sup(D_{V,\mathbb{K}}\not\in \mathbb{N}_{0})$ we say that the space has _infinite dimension_. [[Infinite Dimension Spaces]]
