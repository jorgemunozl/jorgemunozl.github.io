Genre a relative phase. Ref? Move just on the $Z$ axis.
$$
P(\phi)= \begin{bmatrix}
1&0 \\
0&e^{ i\phi }
\end{bmatrix}
$$
Using [[Dirac's bra ket notation]]:
$$
\hat{P}(\phi)\ket{0} =\ket{0} 
$$
$$
\hat{P}(\phi)\ket{1} = e^{ i\phi }\ket{1} 
$$
The geometrical sense is interesting since you are restricted to the $xy$ plane.
$${P}(\theta,\phi)=\ket{\psi_{i}}$$
$$ \ket{\psi_{f}} =\hat{P}(\phi')\ket{\psi_{i}}=P(\theta,\phi+\phi')  $$
So for the specific angles we have:
$$
\begin{align}
\phi & =\pi \to \sigma_{z} \\
\phi & =\frac{\pi}{2}  \to S, S^{2}=\sigma_{z} \\
\phi & =\frac{\pi}{4}  \to T, T^{4}=z, T^{2}=S \\
\phi & =0  \to I\end{align}
$$
