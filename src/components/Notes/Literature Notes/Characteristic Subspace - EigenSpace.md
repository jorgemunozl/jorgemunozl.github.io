>[!definition]
Let $A$ belong to [[Linear Operators Algebra]] , let $\lambda$ be an [[EigenValues|eigenvalue]] of $A$, the space $M_{\lambda}$ called **characteristic subspace** corresponding to $\lambda$ is defined by: [^1]
>$$ M_{\lambda}=\{ f\in V|A(f)=\lambda f \}$$

- Is formed by all the [[Eigenvector]] (and they scalars multiples) that satisfy the eigenvalue equation for a specific lambda.
- If the [[Dimension of a Vector space|dimension]] of one of this spaces is greater that one we call to the eigenvalue associated **degenerated**,if it's one not degenerated. How much freedom we have?
- This set forms a [[Sub Vector Space]] of $V$.
- Is clear that this space is [[Invariant subspace|invariant]] under $A$.
- And  the sum between these spaces is a [[Direct Sum]], because we know that **eigenvectors** are [[Linearly independent]], and this is quite related with the [[Criterions For Diagonalization]].

[^1]: [[Linear Algebra Done Right]] pp. 164
