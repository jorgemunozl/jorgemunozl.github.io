[[lorentz factor]] Mass, Length and Time not are absolute, this depend on our velocity, what?
[[General Relativity Theory MOC]].

The equations could be easily written defined the **Lorentz** **factor**:
$$
\gamma=\frac{1}{\sqrt{ 1-\frac{v^{2}}{c^{2}} }}
$$

If $v$ is close to the [[Speed Of Light]] that factor tends to infinity, but if $v\ll c$ that factor tends to one.

It could be shown that:
1. Respect [[mass, everything have mass]] : $m'=m\gamma$
2. Respect [[time - physics]] : $t'=t / \gamma$
3. Respect length : $L'=L\gamma$
