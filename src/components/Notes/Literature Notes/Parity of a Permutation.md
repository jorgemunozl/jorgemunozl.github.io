The **sign** of a permutation is $1(-1)$ if the permutation is **even** (**odd**). 

**Prop**. A permutation has sign (or parity) $-1$ if the number of [[Permutation|transposition]] of is made it  is **_odd_**. If is **even** $+1$.
$$
sign(\sigma)=\begin{cases}
1 \text{ if even transpositions} \\
-1 \text{ if odd transpositions}
\end{cases}
$$

The interesting is how to prove this. 

>[!example]
Let $(1,3,2)$ a cycle, the determinant associated is:
>$$
P=\begin{vmatrix}
0&0&1\\
1&0&0\\
0&1&0\\
\end{vmatrix}=-1
$$
