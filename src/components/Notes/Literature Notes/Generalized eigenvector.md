>[!definition]
Let $V$ be a [[Vector Space]] over $\mathbb{C}$, $A$ belong to [[Linear Operators Algebra]], $v \in V-\{ 0_{V} \}$ is a **generalized eigenvector** of $A$, correspondent to the [[EigenValues]] $\lambda$ if exist $p \in \mathbb{N}$ such that:
>$$
(A-\lambda id_{v})^{p}(v)=0_{V}
>$$

The generalized characterized sub space correspondent to $\lambda$ is:
$$
K_{\lambda}=\{ v\in V | \exists p\in \mathbb{N},(A-\lambda id_{v})^{p}(v)=0_{V}\}
$$

Is easy to say that every [[Eigenvector]] is a generalized eigenvector.
And to find it we need to one the eigenfirst.
