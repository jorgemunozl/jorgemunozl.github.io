Now when we talk about [[Characteristic Subspace - EigenSpace]] is normal ask, which is the [[Dimension of a Vector space|Dimension]] of this, we call the dimension geometric multiplicity.

>[!definition]
>The **geometric multiplicity** of $\lambda$ [[EigenValues]] is:
>$$ \text{dim}(\text{ker}(A-\lambda I)) $$

This is a geometrical sense. In the other hand the [[Algebraic Multiplicity]]. Is purely algebraic.
