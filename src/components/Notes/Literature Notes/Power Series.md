Is basically a polynomial with infinite grade this means a infinite [[Series]] , nothing else.

A power series is fully determined by choosing a constant c called the **center of the series** and a [[Sequence|sequence]] of numbers $a_{n}$. 

A power series is: 

$$
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_{n}(x-c)
$$

>We make more using a complex sequence and a complex center.
