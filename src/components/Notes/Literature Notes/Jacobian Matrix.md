>[!definition]
When one have a [[Vector field]] $F$ :
>$$
F(x,y,z)=\begin{bmatrix}
F_{1}(x,y,z) \\
F_{2}(x,y,z) \\
F_{3}(x,y,z)
\end{bmatrix}$$
> The **Jacobian Matrix** of this is:
>$$
F(x,y,z)=\begin{bmatrix}
\nabla F_{1} \\
\nabla F_{2} \\
\nabla F_{3}
\end{bmatrix}$$
