$$
T(t)=\frac{f'(t)}{\lVert f'(t) \rVert} =\frac{f'(t)}{\ell'(t)}
$$

That $\ell$ comes from using the [[Arc length parameterization]]:

$$
\ell(t)=\int_{a}^{t}[[f'(\mu)]]d\mu
$$

$$N(t)=\frac{T'(t)}{T(t)}$$

And from this the **Binormal**:
$$B(t)=T(t)\times N(t)$$
