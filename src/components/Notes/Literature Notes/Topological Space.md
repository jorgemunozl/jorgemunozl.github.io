>[!definition]
> A **topological space** is a tuple $(X,\mathcal{T})$, $\mathcal{T}\subset \mathcal{P}(X)$ is called a **topology** such that [^1]:
 >$$
\begin{align}
(i)& \ \emptyset,X\in \mathcal{T} \\
(ii)& A,B\in \mathcal{T} \text{then } A\cap B\in \mathcal{T} \\
(iii)& \{ A_{i} \}_{i\in I}\subset \mathcal{T}, \text{then } \bigcup_{i\in I}A_{i}\in \mathcal{T}
\end{align}$$

- To the elements from $\mathcal{T}$ we called **opens**.
- There are three ways of define a topological space, the three are equivalents. You can choose it depending on the case.
