>The **normal** **distribution** is a distribution of probability [^1] defined like follow:
>$$\mathcal{N}(x|\mu,\sigma^{2})=\frac{1}{ \sqrt{ 2\pi \sigma^{2}}}\exp(-\frac{(x-\mu)^{2}}{2\sigma^{2} })$$
>When $\mu=0$ (mean) ,$\sigma=1$ (deviation) we called Normal, the famous case.
