>[!question]
>Give it a [[Group]], How many [[Subgroup]] could exist? This question is natural because we think a **subgroup** like a ball. Can two of these balls touch it?

This theorem answer to that question:

>[!theorem] 
>Let  $G$ be a group and $S$ a subgroup then [^1]:
>$$
(G:1)=(G:H)(H:1) $$

- This is the index of the group is a multiple of.

[^1]: [[Group Theory]] pp. 17
