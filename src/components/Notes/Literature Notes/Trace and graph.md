The trace of a [[Vector function over a real variable|function]] $f:Dom(f)\to \mathbb{R}^{n}$ is the geometric place notate for notated like $\mathrm{Tr}(f)$ that is basically all the point of the function touch.
$$
\mathrm{Tr}(f)=\{ (f_{1}(t),f_{2}(t),\dots ,f_{n}(t))|t\in Dom(f) \}
$$
The graph is:

$$
Graf(f)=\{ (t,f_{1}(t),f_{2}(t),\dots,f_{n}(t))|t\in Dom(f) \}
$$
This definition tell us more about the graph of the function, we don't want to observe a kind of injection. A false one.
