>[!proposition]
>Let $m,n>0$, $B$ the [[Beta Function]] and $\Gamma$ the [[Gamma Function]], then:
$$\begin{align}B(m,n)=\frac{\Gamma (m) \Gamma(n)}{\Gamma(m+n)}\end{align}$$

- The proof of this relation relies on the [[Convolution Theorem - Faltungs]].
