A [[Matrix what it is]] $A$ is symmetric if: 
$$A=A^{T}$$
Where $A^{T}$ is the [[Transpose of Matrix]]
So an important result is that symmetric matrices are [[Criterions For Diagonalization]] on an [[Orthonormal basis]] basis.

Now important state: All **symmetric matrices** have real [[EigenValues]]
