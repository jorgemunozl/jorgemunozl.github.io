Nothing prohibits us to create a vast quantity of metrics over a set.
To the need of classify this metrics born a way of relate metrics.

>[!tip] How we relate two metrics?
>We say that two metrics $d_{1}$ y $d_{2}$ are metrically equivalent if follow the next relation.(The first to the second) With $\alpha, \beta$ real numbers different of zero. For all point x and y.

ow this relation establish a [[Equivalence Relation]] over the set.

If two metrics are equivalents then $(X,d_{1}),(X,d_{2})$ [[Metric Space]] are metrically equivalent spaces.

Then we know that if we want to work with many metrics is more practice use classes.
