The combination in math and more precisely statistics represents a selection where the order of selection doesn't matter. (When order matters we talk about [[Permutations in Combinatorics]])

>[!example]
>When you are choosing a group to go to the beach, choose first Juan and after Pepe doesn't matter, at the end of the day both are going.

If you have a group for select of $n$ members and you only can choose $p$ members the quantity of different forms that you can make those selections is given by the next formula:

$$
\binom{n}{p}=\frac{!n}{!k\times !(n-k)}
$$

Now exist different _properties_ for this math operator.
