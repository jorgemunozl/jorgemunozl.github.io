> $$\frac{z e^{zx}}{e^{z}-1}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}z^{k}\frac{B_{k}(x)}{!k}$$

$$ B_{n}(x)=\sum_{ k=0}^{n} \binom{n}{k}B_{k}x^{n-k}   $$

- We use $B_{k}$ to refers the [[Bernoulli's Numbers]].
- Now the pretty it's that is you have a good eye this expression remembers to [[Newton Binomial]]. More precisely we can write it like:
$$ B_{n}(x)=(B+x)^{n} $$
