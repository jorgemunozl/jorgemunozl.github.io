>[!theorem]
>$\forall g\in \mathcal{E},\exists!g^{*}\in \mathcal{E}$,$\forall f\in \mathcal{E}$ $(g^{*},f)=(g,A(f))$

>[!definition]
Let $\mathscr{E}$ be an [[Euclid Vector Space]], $A$ a [[Linear Operator]].
The **adjoint** **operator** of $A$  is [^1] $A^{*}$ defined as:
>$$
\begin{align}
A^{*}:\mathscr{E}&\to \mathscr{E} \\
g&\mapsto A^{*}(g)=g^{*} \\
\end{align}$$
>Such that $(g,A(f))=(A^{*}(g),f)$.
