>[!definition]
>Let $S$ and $T$ be [[Sub Vector Space|sub vector spaces]] from the [[Vector Space]] $V$, the **sum** is defined as:
>$$U+V=\{ u+v|u\in U+v\in V \}$$
> And the **intersection** is defined as: [^1]
> $$U\cap V=\{ w|w\in U\land w\in V \}$$

- Both sets forms a [[Sub Vector Space]] of $V$.
- Going one step further also exist the product of spaces $(V\times W)$ [^2], the rest between, well something similar, that is the [[Quotient Space]].
- And amazing way to look at is trough the concept of [[Dimension of a Vector space]], how it look.


[^2]: [[Linear Algebra Done Right]] pp. 110, 61
