When we have a [[Capacitors]] we can introduce a material called **dielectric** between the plaques.
If we apply [[Potential Difference]] to the [[Capacitor Plaques Paralels|capacitor]] we obtain a [[Electric Field]], then the particles of the material are going to **induce** another **Electric Field**.

We are going to call $\vec{E}_{o}$ to the first **Electric Field**. Since the induced electric field is going to have opposite direction, final $\vec{E}$ is going to be less than the first one.

Specifically:
$$
E=E_{o}-E_{\text{ind}}
$$
We define the **Dielectric constant** like:
$$ \frac{E_{o}}{E}=\kappa>1$$
Using the charge density $\sigma$ of each step: [^1]
$$
\sigma_{\text{ind}}=\sigma\left( 1-\frac{1}{\kappa} \right)
$$
- Involved with the [[Electric dipole]]
- We ask about the **superficial density**.
- [[Polarization vector]]
