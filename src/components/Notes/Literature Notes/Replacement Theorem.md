Give it a [[Vector Space]], $G$ [[Span to Generate Spaces]] with $Card(G)=n$, $L \subseteq V$ [[Linearly independent]] with $Card(G)=m$. Then

$$
 1. m\leq n
$$
$$
1. \exists H \subseteq G=n-m \land span(L\cup H)=V
$$

The important of this theorems are its corollary:
- If [[Basis Of A Space Vector (Hamel)|basis]] $B,Card(B)=n$ then all the _basis_ has a $n$ vector
- If the [[Dimension of a Vector space]] is $n$ then all [[Span to Generate Spaces]] with $n$ vector is l.i. then is _basis_.
- If the dimension is $n$ then any _l.i_ set with $n$ vectors is _basis_.
- Every [[Linearly independent]] set, belong to one _basis_.
