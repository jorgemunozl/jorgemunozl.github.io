>[!definition]
A **vector** **field** (or the vector function of a vector) is: $(m,n\in \mathbb{N})$
>$$
\vec{F}:\mathbb{R}^{m}\to \mathbb{R}^{n}
>$$

For $m=n=3$ (The most used on physicis)
$$
F(x,y,z)=(F_{1}(x,y,z),F_{2}(x,y,z),F_{3}(x,y,z))
$$

- Basically we are attaching vectors to a points (another vectors).
- We treat the limits using [[Euclid Norm, product]]
- This is the bigger generalization of a [[Real function]].
- The $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ is the most important case, and the one who care to a physicist.
