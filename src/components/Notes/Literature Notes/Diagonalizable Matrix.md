A matrix it's diagonalizable if exist one [[Basis Of A Space Vector (Hamel)|basis]] $\gamma$  in such a way that the [[Isomorphism matrix and linear maps]] is a diagonal matrix. It's preferible always see the matrix like a [[Linear Map]] 

$$
[A]_{\gamma}=\begin{pmatrix}
\lambda_{1} & 0 & \dots & 0 \\
0 & \lambda_{2} & \dots  & 0 \\
\vdots & \vdots & \ddots &\lambda_{n}
\end{pmatrix}
$$

  Therefore it's necessary know when a matrix could be diagonalizable for that reason was developed the [[Criterions For Diagonalization]].

>This definition of diagonalizable matrix motivates the creation of [[EigenValues]] because for the existence of this matrix we are assuming the that exist vectors that obey the _Eigenvalue equation._
Then all it's based in the necessity to work with matrix more easy to treat and not more easy and the with the sufficient information that the diagonal matrix.
