>[!definition]
**Continuous Case**: [^1]
$$ I=\int r^{2}dm $$
**Discrete Case**: [^2]
$$ I=\sum_{i} r_{i}^{2}\Delta m_{i} $$

- Is like a measure of how many mass exist in a square meter
- And  the beauty of define this is that this is analogous to the mass on all the equation of dynamics and kinematics of a body on movement but on the _rotation_ equation.
- Units: $kg.m^{2}$
- How much well distributed the mass is in the space
- is a measure of how difficult it is to change the rotational motion of an object
- And it's that easy to calculate some moment of inertia of some important figures, for instance rings, circular and rectangles plaques, the theorems that help to solve problems.
