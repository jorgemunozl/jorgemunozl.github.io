Let $f$ a function, $s$ a complex number, the **Laplace** **transform** of $f$ at $s$ is.
$$
\mathcal{L}\{f(t) \}=F(s)=\int_{0}^{\infty} e^{ -st }f(t) \, dt 
$$
This limit exist if the argument of the integral tends to zero in the infinite.
