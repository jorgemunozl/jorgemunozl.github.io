>[!abstract] Definition
>A projection E is a [[Linear Operator]] such that $E \circ E=E$

That is pretty equal to the **projection** **operator**. [[Orthogonal complement]]

From this we could reframe the [[Vector Space]], using projectors and 
$$
E_{M_{j}}=V \to V , f\to E_{M_{j}}(f):=m_{j}
$$
