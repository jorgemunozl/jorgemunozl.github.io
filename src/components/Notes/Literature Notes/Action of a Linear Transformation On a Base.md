>[!proposition]
Any linear map 𝑇 is fully determined by its action on the basis vectors.

Give it a [[Vector Space]] $V$ with $B$ [[Basis Of A Space Vector (Hamel)|basis]], $B=\{ u_{i} \}$. let $v$ be a vector such that $v=\sum \lambda_{i}u_{i}$. A [[Linear Map]] $T:V\to W$.

$$
T(v)=T\left( \sum\lambda_{i}u_{i} \right)=\sum\lambda_{i}T(u_{i})
$$

If we know $T(u_{i})$ we could compute the image of any another vector and the respective [[Matrix representation of map|matrix]].

If exist another _linear map_ $S$ such $S(u_{i})=T(u_{i})$ then $S=T$.
