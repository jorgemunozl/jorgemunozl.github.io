>[!proposition]
>Let $z$ be a complex number, such that $z$ $\notin$ $\mathbb{Z}$ so:
>$$\begin{align}\Gamma(z) \Gamma(1-z)= \frac{\pi}{sin({\pi z})} \end{align}$$
>With $\Gamma$ the [[Gamma Function]].

- In mathematics a **reflection** **formula** establishes relations between a function $f(x)$ and $f(a-x)$. Maybe the most well know is when $a=0$
- This was relation was presented by Leonhard Euler
- To prove this one need to use the [[Weierstrass's Definition of Function Gamma]].
