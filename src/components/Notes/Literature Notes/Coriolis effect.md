The Coriolis effect it's the apparent deflection of moving objects when viewed from a rotating [[Reference Frame, and inertial frame]], It's a direct consequence of the [[Poisson Theorem]].

Coriolis effect plays a significant role in the movement of air masses. Because this don't follow a straight line.
- In the northern hemisphere, air masses are deflected to the right.
- In the southern hemisphere, air masses are deflected to the left.
