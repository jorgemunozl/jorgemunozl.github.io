What the Jacobi Identity seeks to reflect the associative nature of a [[Binary Operation]]. 

>[!tip] Definition
>Let $[]$,$+$ be two binary operations and $\mathbb{0}$ the neutral element.
$$[X,[Y,Z]]+[Y,[Z,X]]+[Z,[X,Y]]=\mathbb{0}$$
>
