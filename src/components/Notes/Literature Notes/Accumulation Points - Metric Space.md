>[!definition]
>Give it a [[Metric Space]] $(X,d)$ , $A\subset X$. A point $x\in A$ is a __accumulation__ point of $A$, if for each open ball with any radius we can find common elements between [[Open , closed balls and spheres|the open ball]] and the subset. This is: $$
\forall r>0 , (B(x,r)-\{ x \})\cap A\neq \emptyset$$

- The point of accumulation is not necessarily has to belong to the subset could be "outside".
- [[Derivative of a set]]
- To the elements that _do not belong to the derivative_ we call __isolated points__. They don't have neighbors. 
 - In the [[Accumulation Point - Real|real case]] we could see that the ball is the all life open set.
