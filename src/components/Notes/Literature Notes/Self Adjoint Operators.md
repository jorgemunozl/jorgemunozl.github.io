>[!definition]
>Give it a [[Vector Space]] $V$ **(finite)**, the [[Linear Operator]] $A$ is [^1] called **Symmetric** if: 
>$$
\langle f,A(g) \rangle =\langle A(f),g \rangle $$

- The [[Adjoint Operator]] $A^{*}$ is equals to $A$.
- The operator $A$ is called **Self Adjoint** if: $A=A^{\dagger}$ and also $Dom(A^{*})=Dom(A)$. [^2]
- [[Eigenvectors of self adjoint operators are orthogonal]].

[^1]: [[Linear Algebra Done Right]] pp. 247

[^2]:  In the finite case if $A$ is **symmetric**, then is **self adjoint** but this is not necessarily true on the infinite case. Acevedo definition.
