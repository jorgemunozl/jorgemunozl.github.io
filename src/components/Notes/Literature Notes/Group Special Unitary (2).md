>[!definition] Set SU(2) Generic Form
> $$SU(2)=\{ \begin{pmatrix}\alpha &-\bar\beta\\\beta & \bar{\alpha}\end{pmatrix}: \alpha,\beta \in \mathbb{C}, \lvert \alpha\rvert ^{2} + \lvert \beta \rvert ^{2}=1 \}$$

>[!definition] Implicit Form
>$$SU(2)=\{U \in \mathbb{C}^{2\times 2 } \ | \ U^{\dagger}U=I , \det(U)=1 \}$$

The [[Group]] $(SU(2),\times)$ where $\times$ is the matrix multiplication. [^1]
 
- The [[Pauli Matrices like Special Unitario (2) Basis]]
- [[Dagger Operator, Conjugate Transpose Matrix]]
- Is really useful in the study its [[SU(2) Lie Algebra|Lie algebra]].
 
[^1]: [[Mathematical Methods for Physicist]] pp.256
