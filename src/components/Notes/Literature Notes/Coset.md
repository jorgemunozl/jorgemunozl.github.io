>[!definition]
>Let $G$ be a [[Group]] and $S$ be a [[Subgroup]] of $G$  then we define the set [^1]:
>$$ aS=\{ as|s\in S \}$$

- When $a$ is in the left of $S$ then we call it a **left coset** the other case **right coset**.
- The **index** is defined as the number of **cosets** that a [[Subgroup]] can accumulate.
- Is like a translation or even a [[Map]] if you want.

[^1]: [[Group Theory]] pp. 17
