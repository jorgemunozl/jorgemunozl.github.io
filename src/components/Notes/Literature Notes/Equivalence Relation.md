>[!definition]
>A **equivalence relation** $(\leq)$ is a [[Binary Relation]] that follow the next axioms: [^1]
>- Reflexivity
>- Symmetry
>- Transitivity

- We can't do much only with the definition of [[Binary Relation]] for that reason we introduce some axioms to have play better, like another fields of math.
- The axiom of transitivity give rise to [[Equivalence Class]]. Since to the eyes of the relation of all those objects are the same (For it).

[^1]: [[Topologia de los espacios metricos]] pp. 12
