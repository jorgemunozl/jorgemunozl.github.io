All liquid is viscous, even water, the air also, but differ in their coefficient. The velocity of the fluid in the cylinder respect the radius is give by.

$$V(r)= \frac{\Delta P}{4 \mu L}(R^2-r^2)$$

What it means is that in the center it moves faster, instead, the closer to the edge it is , the slower it will go.

Which is intuitive.

And we can find the caudal by integrating.

The flow rate, the amount of water (fluid) that pass per unit of time, can be obtained simply by integrating.

$$Q=\pi \frac{\Delta P R^4}{8 \mu L}$$
