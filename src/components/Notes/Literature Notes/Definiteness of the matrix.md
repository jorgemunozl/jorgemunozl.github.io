Let $A$ be a matrix, a square one. Respect the [[EigenValues]]:
- are all positive we say that the matrix is positive definite.
- If all are negative we say that is negative definite.
- If the eigenvalues is zero, (I  know) we say that is semi-definite.

And there is a ton of beauty on this concept.
