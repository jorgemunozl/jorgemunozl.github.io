>[!definition]
>Give it a [[Vector Space]] $V$ over a [[Field]] $\mathbb{K}$, $T$  a [[Linear Map]]. The follow set is known as well as the **dual** **space** of $V$. [^1]
>$$
V^{*}=\{ T\ |\ T:V \to \mathbb{K}\}=Hom(V,\mathbb{K}) $$

- It could be proved that $(V^{*},+,\cdot)$ is a $\mathbb{K}$ vector space. 
- $V^{*}$ is an [[Isomorphism on vectors spaces|Isomorphism]] of $V$, $(V\cong V^{*})$. If $dim(V)\in \mathbb{N}$. If $dim(V) \not\in \mathbb{N}$ then there couldn't exist an _isomorphic._ 
- To the elements of $V^{*}$ are called _linear forms_ or _one-forms_. (Saw the difference with quadratic form)
