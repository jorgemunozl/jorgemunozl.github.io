>[!definition]
Let $(G,g)$ be a [[Group|group]] and $H$ is a set such that $H \subset G$, then we say that $(H,g*)$ is a **subgroup** of $(G,g)$ Where $g*$ is the _restriction_ of $g$ [^1] 
>
>**Restriction**.- If $g$ is the [[Binary Operation]] of the group $(G,g)$, the restriction of $g$ with respect to $H \subset G$ is:
>$$\begin{align}g^*: H \times H& \rightarrow H \\
g^{*}(h,,h)&\mapsto g(h,h)\end{align}$$
- It's better to watch a subgroup like ball inside another bigger ball, and from where one could never escape. (The operator follow is closed). 

[^1]: [[Group Theory]]
