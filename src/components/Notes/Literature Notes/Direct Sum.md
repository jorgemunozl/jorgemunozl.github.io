>[!definition]
>Let $S$ and $T$ be two [[Sub Vector Space]], we say that the [[Operations over a vector space|sum]]set $(S+T)$ is **direct** if $S\cap T=\{ \emptyset \}$. In that case we notate 
>$S\oplus T$.

- A set of this is [[Linearly independent]]
- It could act like a pseudo [[Basis Of A Space Vector (Hamel)]]; unique coeficients.
- [[External Direct Sum]]
- [[Direct Sum of Linear Maps]] 

>[!theorem]
>If $V$ is **direct sum** of the $K$ sub spaces if and only if the sum of sub space is $V$ and the sum of dimension of the sub spaces is equal to the dimension of $V$.
