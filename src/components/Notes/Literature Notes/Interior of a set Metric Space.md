>[!definition]
>Let  be $A\subset X$ , $(X,d)$ be a [[Metric Space]], a point $x\in A$ is called **interior** **point** if: [^1]
>$$ \exists r_{x}\in \mathbb{R}|B(x,r_{x})\subset A$$
>The **interior** of $A$ notated as $\overset{\circ}{\text{A}}$ is the set formed by all the **interior** **point**.

- Of course we want to differentiate the frontier of a set and its border.
- In the reals we can be less specific. [[Interior of a Real]]
- The [[Open , closed balls and spheres|open ball]] radius could be extremely small, infinitesimal.

[^1]: [[Topologia de los espacios metricos]] pp. 40
