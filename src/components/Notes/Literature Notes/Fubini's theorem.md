>[!theorem]
>Let $f$ be a [[Scalar function]], then [^1]:
>$$
\iint f(x,y)dxdy
=\iint f(x,y)dydx$$

- This theorem states that the [[Double Integral]] do not care the order.
- When the integral form is very complicated or is not very obvious is very useful.
- Here what matter is well pretty the region where we want to integrate. [[Double Integral Resolution]]

[^1]: [[Analisis Matematico III Calculo Vectorial]]
