>[!tip] Criterion
>If for all $n$ the follow is true:
 $$\lvert  \frac{a_{n+1}}{a_{n}} \rvert\leq c<1$$

- Then the [[Series]] $\sum a_{n}$ is [[Absolutely Convergent Series]].
- Is clear that if the quotient is greater than one the series diverges. [^1]

[^1]: [[Analisis Real Elon Lages]] pp. 58
