>[!definition]
>A partition over the interval $[a,b]$ is a finite subset of points $P=\{ t_{0},t_{1},\dots,t_{n} \}\subset[a,b]$ [^1].
In such a way that $a=t_{0}<t_{1}<t_{2}<\dots <t_{n}=b$. The interval $[t_{i-1},t_{i}]$, of length $t_{i}-t_{i-1}$ it's called _i-th interval_ of the partition $P$. 


- Obviously $\sum_{i=1}^{n} (t_{i}-t_{i-1})=b-a$
- The question is, we could generalize this using a [[Metric Space]].
