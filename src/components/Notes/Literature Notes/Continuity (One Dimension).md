>[!definition]
>Let $f$ be a [[Real function]], is continue if: [^1]
> $$ \forall \epsilon > 0 \exists \delta >0 : x \in D \land |x-a|<\delta\implies |f(x)-f(a)|<\epsilon $$

>If the function do not follow this requirements we say that the function is discontinue, exist three main [types of discontinuity](kindcont.png).

There are sub topics like: Algebra of continue functions, Relation between continuity and [[Sequence]] Courtesy of the Continuity.

Pretty [theorems](TeoremasdeContinuidad.png) are Mean value theorem for continuity [[Continuity (Many Dimension)|that are generalizable]] are: [[Extreme Value Theorem]].
