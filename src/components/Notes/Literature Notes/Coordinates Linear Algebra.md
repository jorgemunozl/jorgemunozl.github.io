>[!definition]
Give it a [[Vector Space]] $V$, and a well define a [[Basis Of A Space Vector (Hamel)|ordered basis]] $\{ e_{i} \}$. The coordinates of $v\in V$ are the numbers $a_{i}$ such that $v=\sum a_{i}e_{i}$. The _coordinates vector (respect the basis) is_: 
>$$
[f]_{\beta}=\begin{bmatrix}
a_{1} \\
a_{2} \\
\vdots \\
a_{n}
\end{bmatrix}$$

- It's clear that exist and [[Isomorphism on vectors spaces|isomorphism]] between $V$ and $\mathbb{K}^{n}$ both over the [[Field]] $\mathbb{K}$. This take the vector to its _coordinates vector_.
- Is like the identity document of respect a basis, is really useful describe vector trough its coordinates. There are cases where describe a vector lonely could be very hard because it's abstraction. When talking about [[tensor propierties|tensors]] this is quite important.
