Called **one-particle orbitals**.

This is something tricky to understand.

They come from [[Quantum Numbers]], but they are symbols, how you work mathematically with them.

They are the [[Stationary States for the Schrodinger Equation]] for the [[Hidrogen Atom]] and an approximation for the others. the famous [[Spherical Harmonics]].

---
So here is the spell out: Single particle: Pretty clear what does it means
$$
\psi(x)=\psi(\mathbf{r},s)
$$
Where: $\psi$ lives in the Hilbert Space.

Now we combine spin orbitals: Spatial and spin, via a single product.

$$
\phi_{p}(\mathbf{r})\omega_{p}(s)=\chi_{p}(x)
$$
Chemist call this $\chi$ one particle orbitals. Each column is a spin orbital.
