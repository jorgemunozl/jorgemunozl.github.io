Let $X\subset \mathbb{R}$, we call a [[Accumulation Point - Real]] by the left, this is $a\in X'_{+}$ when: [[Lateral Accumulation Points On The Reals]]
$$ \forall \varepsilon>0, X\cap(a,a+\varepsilon)\neq \emptyset $$
