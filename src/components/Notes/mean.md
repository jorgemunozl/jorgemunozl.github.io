In the most general case the **mean** is defined via the [[Expected Value]] operator [^1].

>[!definition]
>Give it a [[Random variable (univariate)|random variable]] $X$ its mean is defined by:
>$$ \mathbb{E}_{X}(x)=\int_{X}xp(x)dx $$

- We can see it more clearly when is discrete and the probability is uniform, summing fractions.

[^1]: [[Mathematics for Machine Learning]] pp. 193
