>[!definition]
>A capacitor is a physical object that keeps charge, and the most important of it is:
>$$  C\equiv \frac{Q}{\Delta V} $$

- Where the value of $C$ depends completely on the geometry and the intrinsic material of the materials that you are using. Similar to a [[Inductors]].
- With $Q$ [[Charge]], where $C$ is called the **capacitance** and is a constant and $\Delta V$ is the [[Potential Difference]].
- [[Capacitor Plaques Paralels]]
- The second equal comes from the fact of the capacitors are like two planes  the electric field on a plane is lke $\frac{\sigma}{\varepsilon_{0}}$  that with the fact of [[Potential Uniform Electric Field]] $\Delta V=Ed$, and assuming that the plane is a square then you have it.
- It could be considered a [[Dielectric Material Constant]]
