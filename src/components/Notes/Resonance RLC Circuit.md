If we look a vanilla [[RLC circuits]] , it's clear that energy is going to decay, because the [[Resistors]] is going to transform that energy from the [[Electromotive Force]] into heat. If we don't want that happens then we can add a [[Alternating Current]] to fight against that loose of energy. 

The **AC** frequency can't be any value, but a specific one such that we can consider that the resistor is not there. 

No the definition is when the $I_{\text{RMS}}$ is maximum.
