A ordered field is when you first establish a [[Binary Relation]] and that order relation follow the condition: [^1]
- The **relation** is total, just one of this is true: 
- The **relation** is compatible with the operations $+,\times$ of the [[Field]].

Now we can talk about the **Arquimidiean property**.

$$
\forall x\in \mathbb{F},\exists n\in \mathbb{N}|n>x
$$

If you flip it:

$$
\forall x\in \mathbb{F},\exists n\in \mathbb{N}|\frac{1}{n}<x
$$

[^1]: [[Analisis Real Elon Lages]] pp. 27
