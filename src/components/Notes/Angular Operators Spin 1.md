I wonder how is relate to the [[Angular Momentum Operator]].
This is not the definition itself, recall that. [^2]
The question is that this guy is defined on the [[Hilbert Space Spin 1]] and the forms are: [^1]

$$
L_{+}=\hbar\begin{pmatrix}
0 & \sqrt{ 2 } & 0 \\
0 & 0 & \sqrt{ 2 } \\
0 & 0 & 0
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
L_{-}=\hbar\begin{pmatrix}
0 & 0 & 0 \\
\sqrt{ 2 } & 0 & 0 \\
0 & \sqrt{ 2 } &  0
\end{pmatrix}
$$
It's clear that $L_{-}^{\mathsf{\top}}=L_{+}$. And interesting fact is:
$$
L_{\pm}=L_{x}\pm iL_{y}
$$

Where $\hbar$ is the [[Reduced Planck Constant]]. The operator $L_{-}$ is $L_{+}^{\mathsf{\top}}$.

$$
L_{z}=\hbar\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 0  & -1
\end{pmatrix}
$$
