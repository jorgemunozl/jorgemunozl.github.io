>[!definition]
>Let $f$ be a real [[Real function]], with domain $D$, and $a$ an [[Accumulation Point - Real|accumulation point]] of domain, if $L$ is the limit on the point $a$. Then: [^1]
  $$\lim_{ x \to a }f(x)=L \implies$$
  $$\forall \epsilon>0 \exists \delta >0 / x \in D \land 0<|x-a|<\delta \implies |f(x)-L|<\epsilon  $$
