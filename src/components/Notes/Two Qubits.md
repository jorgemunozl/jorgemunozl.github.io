So the computational base is: (From the [[Qubits, main piece of Quantum Computing|qubit definition]]). But wait this come from the [[Direct Kronecker Product]].
$$ \ket{00} =\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix} ,\ket{01} =\begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}, \ket{11} =\begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}, \ket{10} =\begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix} $$

Or also:

$$
\begin{align}
\ket{00} = \ket{0} \otimes \ket{0}  \\
\ket{01} = \ket{0} \otimes \ket{1}  \\
\ket{11} = \ket{1} \otimes \ket{1}  \\
\ket{10} =\ket{1} \otimes \ket{0} 
\end{align}
$$
