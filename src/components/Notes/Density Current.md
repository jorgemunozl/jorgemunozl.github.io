We have that the [[Current Intensity]] is:

$$ I=nqv_{d}A$$

Then we define the scalar value $J$.

$$ J=\frac{I}{A} $$

- Recall that we are considering that direction of the current is perpendicular to the current direction and the current is uniform.

And is useful consider it like a vector.

$$ \vec{J}=\frac{d\vec{I}}{dS_{n}} $$

Now the direction is always equal that the [[Electric Field]].

And some special case: [[Ohm Law]]
