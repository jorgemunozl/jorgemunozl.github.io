When you have a function rationale of sines and cosines use the follow substitution to solve that [[integral]]. 

[[Change of variable one variable]]

$$
u=\arctan\left( \frac{x}{2} \right)
$$
So you can rewrite easily $\cos(u)$ and $\sin(u)$
