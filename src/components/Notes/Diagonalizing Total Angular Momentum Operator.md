A result that you actually care is:
$$
\mathcal{H}_{\frac{1}{2}}\otimes \mathcal{H}_{1}=\mathcal{H}_{\frac{3}{2}}\oplus \mathcal{H}_{\frac{1}{2}}
$$
The [[tensor product]] of the [[Hilbert Space Spin]] is the [[Direct Sum]] of another spaces with different spin.
That comes from the fact of:
$$
J_{z}^{3/2}\oplus J_{z}^{1/2}
$$
Where $J_{z}^{3/2}$ is the matrix representation of $J$ in the basis $\mathcal{B}_{3/2}$. The same for the another guy.
