Here we use the **arc length** of the curve (we should demand the differentiation?) to _re-parameterize_ one _curve_.

We create the [[Vector function over a real variable|function]] $\hat{f}=f\circ s$, where $s$ is the [[Arc length|arc length function]].
It's easy to observe that the domain of $\hat{f}$ is $[0,L]$, the range of $s$.

The beauty of this [[Re-parameterization]] is that we obtain.

$$
\lVert  \hat{f}(\tau) \rVert =1, \forall \tau \in D_{\hat{f}}
$$

It could be proved that is the unique _re-parameterization_ such that the norm is equal to zero.

Think it like ball of constant velocity going trough for a string. In this video of Bezier curves there is a good visual explanation, and I guess that this has direct application in the video games creation.
