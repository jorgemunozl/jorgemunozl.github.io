>[!definition]
>Let $A$ be a matrix of order $n\times n$ and the $A_{ij}$ the cofactor from $a_{ij}$. Then the cofactor matrix is: [^1]
$$
\text{cof}(A)=\begin{bmatrix}
A_{11} & A_{12} & \dots A_{1n} \\
A_{21}  & A_{22}  &  \\

\end{bmatrix}
$$

We can obtain $A_{ij}$ by calculating the [[Determinant of a matrix]] from a matrix which is $n-1\times n-1$. And take in account the position, if it's negative or positive.
