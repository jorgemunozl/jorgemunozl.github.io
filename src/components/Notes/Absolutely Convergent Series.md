>[!definition]
>Let $\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}a_{k}$ be a [[series]]. This is **absolutely convergent** if the **series** 
>$$
\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\lvert a_{k} \rvert 
>$$ 
>is [[Convergence In Series|convergent]].
- It's clear that if $\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\lvert a_{k} \rvert$ converges then $\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} a_{k}$ (Prove it) The norm tends to zero. 
- The other way around is not necessary true. In that case we called **conditionally convergent**. [^1]
- The [[Harmonic Series]] multiply by $(-1)^{n}$ is conditionally **convergent**. [^2]
