>[!note] 
>Is the generalization of the fact that the rationales are dense on the reals and all the stuff.
>I guess that it is a fact well know for a long time.

Give a [[Metric Space]], ($X,d$) , $A\subset X$ it is call dense on X if: $\overline{A}=X$.

>[!question] What this mean?

The [[Closure of a set|closure]] of A, this is the set and all its _accumulation points_ form the entire set.

What better than an example, we know that the rational are dense on the reals, I imagine the real line with infinites open balls, the rational with its accumulation points form the real line.
