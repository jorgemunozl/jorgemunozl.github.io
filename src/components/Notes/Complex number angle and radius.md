>For every $z$ that belongs to the [[Complex Numbers]], $z$ could be written as:
>$$z = \lvert z \rvert e^{ i\theta },\theta \in \mathbb{R}$$.

- This is super strong. Why $e$ have that special property, to represent a circle with radius one.
