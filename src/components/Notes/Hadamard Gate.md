So take a [[Qubits, main piece of Quantum Computing]] and transform it into a the opposite one.

$$
H=\frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2 }}\begin{bmatrix}
1&1 \\
1&-1
\end{bmatrix}
$$

So in the [[Bloch sphere have any geometrical sense]], we have that:

$$
\ket{z,+} \to \ket{x,+} 
$$

$$
\hat{H}\ket{0} =\frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2 }}(\ket{0} +\ket{1} )
$$

$$
\hat{H}\ket{1} =\frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2 }}(\ket{0} -\ket{1} )
$$

It is a [[Unitary Operator]].
