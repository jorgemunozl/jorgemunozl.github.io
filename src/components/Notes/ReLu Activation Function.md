One of the most famous [[Activation function]].

Think of like: Take a vector and replace all the negative values for zeros.

 $ReLU$ - (Rectified Linear Unit) - $max(0,x)$

The derivative is:

$$
f'=\begin{cases}
1 & x>0 \\
0 & x<0 
\end{cases}
$$
