Sample Mean
$$
\overline{X}=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}X_{i}$$ 
Variance
$$
s^{2}=\frac{1}{n-1}\sum_{i=1}^{n}(X_{i}-\overline{X})^{2}
$$
Maximum Likelihood Estimator (MLE) Condition
$$\frac{d}{d\theta}\log L(\theta;X_{1},\dots ,X_{n})=0 
$$
Cumulative Distribute Function (CDF)
$$
F(x)=P(X\leq x)
$$
Expected Value (Continue Case)
$$
\mathbb{E}[X]=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} xf(x) dx 
$$
Variance Formula (using expectation)
$$
Var(X)=\mathbb{E}[X^{2}]-(\mathbb{E}[X])^{2}
$$
Covariance
$$
\text{Cov}(X,Y)=\mathbb{E}[(X-\mu_{X})(Y-\mu_{Y})]
$$
Correlation Coefficient 
$$
\rho_{XY}=\frac{\text{Cov}(X,Y)}{\sigma _{X}\sigma_{Y}}
$$
