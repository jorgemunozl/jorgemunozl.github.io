The [[Nabla Operator]] is the Spherical System is:

$$
\nabla=\hat{\mathbf{r}}\frac{\partial }{\partial r}+\boldsymbol{\hat{\theta}}\frac{1}{r}\frac{\partial }{\partial \theta}+\hat{\boldsymbol{\phi}}\frac{1}{r\sin \theta}\frac{\partial }{\partial \phi}
$$
