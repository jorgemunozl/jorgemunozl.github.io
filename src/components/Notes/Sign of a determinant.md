The sign of a determinant is a conditioned by the absolute value of the **Determinant**.

$$
\text{sign}(\det(A))= \begin{cases}
1 \text{ if }\det(A)>0 \\
-1 \text{ if } \det(A)<0 \\
0 \text{ otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$
Then we can write something like:
$$
\det(A)=\text{sign}(A)\cdot \exp(\log |\det A|)
$$
So it can be positive negative or zero. [[Parity Law]], I mean [[Orientation of A Vector Space]].
