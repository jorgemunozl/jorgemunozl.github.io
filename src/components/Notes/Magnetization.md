Each particle has its own [[Magnetic Moment]] called $\vec{\mu}$. So the total magnetization of a particle with a ton of moment induced by a external magnetic field is the number of particles $N$ times $\vec{\mu}$.

$$
\vec{M}=N\vec{\mu}
$$

But how I can approximate the number of particles $N$?

Using the fact from chemistry that:

$$
\frac{N}{N_{A}}=\frac{m}{\overline{M}}=\frac{\rho V}{\overline{M}}
$$
Where $\overline{M}$ is the [[Molar Mass]], $N_{A}$ is the [[Avogadro Number]], $\rho$ the [[density]]  and $V$ the volume of the particle.
