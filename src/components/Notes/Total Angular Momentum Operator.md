But in a certain sense this comes from the [[Angular Momentum]].
Definition of the momentum  based on the [[commutator operator]]. [^1]
This are consequence of the definition
$$ 
\hat{J}_{\pm}\ket{j,m} =\hbar \sqrt{ j(j+1)-m(m\pm 1) }\ket{j,m\pm 1}  $$
For $z$ we obtain:
$$
\hat{J}_{z}\ket{j,m} =\hbar m\ket{j,m} 
$$
And you can compactly write:
$$
\hat{J}_{\pm},z=S_{\pm,z}\otimes \mathbb{I} +\mathbb{I}\otimes \hat{L}_{\pm,z}
$$
Where $\hat{S}$ is the action operator from [[Hilbert Space Spin 1 2]] and 
[[Diagonalizing Total Angular Momentum Operator]]

[^1]: [[Quantum Mechanics Zettili]] pp. 306
