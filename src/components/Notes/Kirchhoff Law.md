Measure how many [[Current Intensity]] per your resistor and how measure the [[Potential (Voltage)]] in each [[Resistors]].
$$ V_{a}+\sum IR=V_{b} $$
The sign is a important matter, you have to consider the follow: (For mesh, because at the end you sum up zero)
- If the direction of the current go **against** of the direction of the potential that you consider then is **positive**.
- if the current goes in the **same** direction then is **negative**. [^1]

- Label the nodes. (ABCDE)
- Choose an arbitrary path for the nodes. Current.
- Apply the Kirchhoff law.
- Create a [[system equation]] to obtain each $I$. And that's it.
