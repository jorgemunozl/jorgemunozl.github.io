So what is this? I mean you have a [[Markov Chain]] and then you apply monte carlo?
How another methods different from [[Metropolis Hasting algorithm]] works.


>[!definition]
>A Markov chain is Probability distribution a which only depends on the last state, not on the historial of states. This is: [^1]

$$
P(a|o_{N},o_{N-1},\dots ,o_{1})=P(a|o_{N})
$$



- That idea makes it very good for approximate the chaos of the universe.
