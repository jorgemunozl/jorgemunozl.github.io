The [[Hadamard Gate]] can take the [[Pauli Matrices]] and return also [[Pauli Matrices]].
This is in a another way a group with [[Pauli Matrix Z]], [[Pauli Matrix Y]] and [[Pauli Matrix X]].

$$
\begin{align}
HZH & =X \\
HXH & =Z \\
HYH & =-Y
\end{align}
$$
