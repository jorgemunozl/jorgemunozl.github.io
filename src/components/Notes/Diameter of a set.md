>[!note]
>The analogous of find two points of a subset and the distance of these point be the biggest. Do not exist two another points that its distance overcome this.

Give it a [[Metric Space]] and a subset, the diameter ($\partial$) of this subset is the maximum real value representing the distance of two points. 

Obviously this exist if the family of reals numbers that represents the distance between two points in the subset is bounded.

>Then how is bounded exists the diameter ($\partial \in \mathbb{R}$) and we call to the ___bounded___ subset.
