$\mathcal{H}_{\frac{1}{2}}$ the [[Hilbert Space]] for particles with spin 1/2.

This space has dimension two with the basis:

$$
\ket{\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}}=\ket{+}=\begin{pmatrix}
1 \\
0
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
\ket{\frac{1}{2},-\frac{1}{2}} =\ket{-} =\begin{pmatrix}
0 \\
1
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Now we want operators in this space: [^1] This operators comes from the [[General Theory of Spin]]

$$
S_{+}=\hbar \begin{pmatrix}
0 & 1 \\
0  & 0
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
S_{-}=\hbar \begin{pmatrix}
0 & 0 \\
1 & 0
\end{pmatrix}
$$
$$
S_{z}=\frac{\hbar}{2}\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 \\
0 & -1
\end{pmatrix}
$$

So I think that we can use a special notation (using $j,m$) for this specific case is clear that:

$$
j,m \in \left\{  \frac{1}{2},-\frac{1}{2}  \right\}
$$

Thus we can extract the $m$ with the $S_{z}$ operator:
$$
S_{z}\ket{j,m}=\hbar m\ket{j,m}  
$$


[^1]: [[Quantum Mechanics Zettili]] pp.315
