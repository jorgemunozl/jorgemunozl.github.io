Consider a conductor cylindrical with area transversal $A$, let's take a $\Delta x$, the amount of charges $(n)$ that pass trough it with a [[Crawl Speed]].

Let $\eta$ be the number of electron $n$ per volume $V$:
$$ \eta=\frac{n}{V} $$
By using the definition of **volume**:
$$
\eta A\Delta x=n
$$
Let $q$ be the charge of the electron, or a single particle.
$$ \Delta Q=(\eta A\Delta x)q $$
But: $\Delta x=v_{d}\Delta t$ thus:
$$ I_{avg}=\frac{\Delta Q}{\Delta t}=nqv_{d}A $$
Look that if we divide by the area $A$, we obtain the [[Density Current]].
