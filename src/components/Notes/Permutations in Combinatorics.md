This represents the selections of elements from a set, where order matters.
A _permutation_ (in this context) is a specifies grouping, a determined arrange of elements of the set. 

>[!example] 
>If you are going to decide the members of a neighborhood council, you have to decide who is going to be the mayor, the secretary and the spokesman. Here matter a lot the order, because exist hierarchy, order, relevance, sequence ,priorities.

Now give it a set with $n$ elements the number of all its permutations is given by the [[Factorial]] in the follow way.

$$
V(n)=!n
$$

It's the same of _factorial_ but here you give context, like an application.
Or if you want the definition of factorial.
