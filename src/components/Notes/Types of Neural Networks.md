Different types of neural networks perform in specific task, we can see the big differences between one to each other by its _architecture_ and the _algorithms_ involved to find the _weights and bias_.

 - **Feed forward Neural Networks (FNNs)** →  The process is straightforward  , the inputs convert into the output [[FFN working|like water flowing]].
- **Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs)** → Have feedback loops, good for sequential data.
- **Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)** → Designed for [[Convolution Neural Network|image processing]].
- **Hopfield Networks & Boltzmann Machines** → [[Hopfield Networks|Energy-based models]] models with feedback.
