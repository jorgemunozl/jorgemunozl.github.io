>[!definition]
> Give it a [[conductor]] where exist [[Current Intensity]] the **current density** is define as [^1] [^2]:
>$$J\equiv \frac{I}{A}=nqv_{d}$$
> Where $I$ is the current and $A$ is the transversal area and $n=\frac{\#port}{V}$ represents the density of charge, $q$ the [[Charge]] flowing and $v_{d}$ the [[Crawl Speed]].

- The second equation is consequence of the definition
- It have unit **Ampere per square meter**.
- It also exist the differential form: $\frac{dI}{dS_{n}}$
- You can think it like a vector with direction equal to the flux of the positives charges. Why?
- The third equal comes from the fact that $n=\frac{port}{vol}=\frac{port}{Area\times dx}=\frac{port}{area\times vd\times t}$
- Because $\Delta Q=num port*q$

[^1]: [[Fisica para ciencias e ingenieria]] pp. 694

[^2]: [[Physics III - 2025 II]] Session seven one.
