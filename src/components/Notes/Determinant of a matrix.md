A **determinant** it's an _application_, $\lvert \cdot \rvert:\mathbb{K}_{n\times n}\to \mathbb{K}$, [[Matrix what it is]] $\mathbb{K}$ square.

Here we have to represent to the matrices trough its column vector, this follow two properties: [^1]
1. $\lvert \cdot \rvert$ is n-lineal.
2. $\lvert \cdot \rvert$ is alternated. (From where I get that reference?)
$$ \det(A)=\sum_{\sigma \in S_{n}} sgn(\sigma)\prod_{i=1}^{n}a_{i,\sigma(i)} $$

- This is more a mathematical definition the one that physics give us, the [[Cross product]].
- Definition formal using [[Permutations in Combinatorics|permutations]] (century XIX). 
- Where $sgn$ represent the [[Parity of a Permutation]]
