>[!definition]
$\vec{\nabla}$ is a [[Differential Operator]]. It's composed by the [[Partial derivative|partial derivatives]] of a [[Scalar function]].
$$
\nabla =\left( \frac{\partial }{\partial x },\frac{\partial }{\partial y}, \frac{\partial }{\partial z } \right)
$$
And for $g:\mathbb{R}^{n}\to \mathbb{R}$. **Gradient**.

$$ \nabla=\left[ \frac{\partial g}{\partial x_{0}},\frac{\partial g}{\partial x_{1}},\frac{\partial g}{\partial x_{2}},\frac{\partial g}{\partial x_{3}},\dots \frac{\partial g}{\partial x_{n} } \right] $$
Some important applications are:
- [[Gradient]]
- [[Divergence - Nabla Operator]]
- [[Curl]]
- [[Laplacian]]
- [[Nabla Operator on Differents Coordinate Systems]]
