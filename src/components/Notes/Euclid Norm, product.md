Let be $\mathbf{v},\mathbf{u}\in\mathbb{R}^{n}$. The scalar dot is:

$$
\langle \mathbf{v},\mathbf{u} \rangle=v_{i}u_{i} 
$$

This defines a [[Norm]] on $\mathbb{R}^{n}$:

$$
\lVert \mathbf{u} \rVert =\sqrt{ \langle \mathbf{u},\mathbf{u} \rangle   }
$$

And also a [[Metric Space]]:

$$
d(\mathbf{u,v})=\lVert \mathbf{u-v} \rVert 
$$

Say to us the level of similitude between two vectors.

$$
\frac{\langle \mathbf{u,v} \rangle}{\lVert \mathbf{u} \rVert\lVert \mathbf{v} \rVert  } =\cos(\theta) 
$$

And I don't know who was the first who define it but I believe the reason to define it like this is for [[Pythagorean Theorem]].
