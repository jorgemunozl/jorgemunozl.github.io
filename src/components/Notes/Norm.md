>[!definition]
>A **norm** is a [[Function]] $\lVert \cdot \rVert:X\to \mathbb{R}^{+}$ which obeys the[^1].
>1. Non negativity.
>2. Scale (Homogeniety)
>3. Triangle of inequality

- A [[Vector Space]] with **Norm** is called **Normed Vector Space**.
- Not every norm comes from an [[Inner product]].

>[!example]
**Minkowski Norm**
>$$
\lVert x \rVert_{p}=\left( \sum_{i=1}^{n} \lvert x _{i}\rvert ^{p} \right)^{\frac{1}{p}}  $$

[^1]: [[Linear Algebra Done Right]] pp. 186
