Defined as[^1]:
$$
\zeta(x)=\log(1+\exp(x))
$$

The derivative which is important for us:

$$
\zeta'(x)=\frac{1}{\exp(-x)+1}
$$
Is clear that this function is always positive. And it's like a smooth [[ReLu Activation Function]].
