First let's define the space $\mathcal{H}_{1}$, the [[Hilbert Space]] for the particle spin 1. Whatever that mean.

With this basis: 
$$
\ket{1,m} =\{ \ket{1,1} ,\ket{1,0} ,\ket{1,-1}  \}
$$

With:

$$
\ket{1,1} =\begin{pmatrix}
1 \\
0 \\
0
\end{pmatrix},\ket{1,0} =\begin{pmatrix}
0 \\
1 \\
0
\end{pmatrix}, \ket{1,-1} =\begin{pmatrix}
0 \\
0 \\
1
\end{pmatrix}
$$
