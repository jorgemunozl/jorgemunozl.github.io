For training the pretty famous model ChatGPT, openAI used 10000 humans (probably from India or one of these countries with a ton people searching for a job) giving feedback to the Chatgpt responses.
They were sitting a ton of hours saying yes or no to the responses, and the model can update their parameters to sound better, to sound more like a human, because they are (for its architecture) a pretty fancy predictor of the next word.
