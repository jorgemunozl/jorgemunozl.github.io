> [!definition]
> The reward function is $r(\mathbf{o}_{t},\mathbf{a}_{t})$, basically a signal from the environment.

There is the reward associated to a [[Trajectory RL]].
$$
R(\tau)=\sum_{t=0}^{T}r_{t}
$$
Here we could introduce the **discount factor gamma**, $\gamma$.
