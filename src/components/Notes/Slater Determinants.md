As consequence of the [[Fermi Dirac Statistics]], and all that.
We could see it like a a more flexible [[Hartree Product]]
We have the follow determinant [^1].

$$
\Phi=\begin{vmatrix}
\psi_{n_{1}}(\xi_{1}) & \psi_{n_{1}}(\xi_{2}) \\
\psi_{n_{2}}(\xi_{1}) & \psi_{n_{2}}(\xi_{2})
\end{vmatrix}
$$


We parameterize the many-electron wavefunction             with an attention-based Slater–Jastrow ansatz (PsiFormer) and optimize it using variational Monte Carlo.
